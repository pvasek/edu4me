# Geografie – syllabus

From "where am I and how do I find the way?" to the level expected at the Czech maturita and at university entrance (A-level / AP Human Geography). **12 levels, 85 lessons** (6–8 per level), plus a level test and mini-games in every level. Czech only; levels 1–9 for ZŠ (age 12–15, *zeměpis*, from 2026 *geografie*), levels 10–12 for gymnázium (age 15–19, *geografie*).

**Mathematics.** Ratios and map scale, percentages, reading and drawing graphs (klimatogram, population pyramid, hydrograph), averages and ranges on ZŠ; on gymnázium growth rates and doubling time, density and indices (HDI, Gini in plain words), simple correlation from scatter plots. Coordinates in degrees and minutes; time zones as 15° per hour. No trigonometry beyond what the maths course has.

### Principles for the order

1. **Map first.** Every later lesson uses maps, coordinates, scale and contour lines, so level 1 teaches the tools before any content.
2. **The planet before its surface.** Shape, rotation and revolution (level 2) explain day and night, seasons and climate belts, which level 4 needs.
3. **Systematic before regional.** Relief, climate, water and biomes (levels 3–4), people (level 5) and the economy (level 6) come before the regions of the world (levels 7–9), so every region is described with tools the learner already has. Czech ŠVPs often teach the continents in 7th grade before population and economy; we reorder because a region cannot be explained without them.
4. **From the world to my place.** World regions → Europe → Czechia → my municipality (fieldwork, z9-7).
5. **Spiral: ZŠ describes, gymnázium explains.** Each big area is met twice: relief 3 → 10, climate 4 → 10, population and cities 5 → 11, economy and development 6 → 12, sustainability 9 → 12.
6. **Observe, measure, explain.** Lessons start from a photo, a map or a measured value (temperature, a river level, a census figure), then a graph, then the model.
7. **Hazards and sustainability close each stage.** They integrate physical and human geography, so they come last (z4-7, z9-6, levels 10 and 12).

The emblem of a level is a record holder of planet Earth (the prime meridian, the summit farthest from Earth's centre, the highest mountain, the deepest lake, the most densely populated state, the shortcut between two oceans, the largest river, the river through the most countries, Czechia's highest peak, the deepest place, the smallest state, the coldest continent). ## Detailed syllabus

Each lesson lists its **core content**. Lessons must cover every bullet and must not use concepts from later lessons without saying so.

### Level 1 – Mapa a orientace
*Goal: read any map and find the way with a map, a compass and a phone.*

- **z1-1 Co je geografie a jak se ptá** – geography as „kde a proč tam"; physical vs human geography; place, space, region; the geographer's questions and sources (map, photo, statistics, fieldwork); mental map of one's own surroundings.
- **z1-2 Glóbus a zeměpisná síť** – the globe as a model; poles, equator, meridians and parallels; the prime meridian (Greenwich); latitude and longitude in degrees and minutes, N/S, E/W; reading and writing coordinates; worked examples (Prague 50° 05′ s. š., 14° 25′ v. d.).
- **z1-3 Mapa a měřítko** – map vs plan vs globe; numerical and graphical scale; large vs small scale; computing real distances from the map and back, with units (cm → km); worked examples; why scale limits detail (generalisation).
- **z1-4 Mapové značky a výškopis** – legend, point/line/area symbols; altitude (nadmořská výška) and relative height; contour lines (vrstevnice) and their interval; colour hypsometry; reading slopes, hills and valleys from contours; drawing a simple profile.
- **z1-5 Orientace v krajině** – cardinal and intermediate directions; orienting a map; compass and buzola, magnetic vs geographic north (in plain words); azimuth; orientation by the Sun and by landmarks; safe hiking (turistické značky KČT).
- **z1-6 Druhy map a jejich zkreslení** – thematic vs general maps; the atlas and its index; why every flat map distorts (area, shape, distance, direction); Mercator vs equal-area projections; Greenland vs Africa; choosing a map for a purpose.
- **z1-7 Digitální mapy, GPS a GIS** – web maps (Mapy.cz), navigation; GPS satellites and position; satellite and aerial images, remote sensing; GIS as layers of data on one map; open data (ČÚZK, ČSÚ); everyday uses and limits.

### Level 2 – Země ve vesmíru
*Goal: explain day and night, seasons, time zones and tides from Earth's shape and motions.*

- **z2-1 Tvar a velikost Země** – evidence that Earth is round (ships, eclipses, photos from space); geoid and oblateness in plain words; dimensions (circumference ≐ 40 000 km, radius ≐ 6 371 km); Eratosthenes' measurement as a worked example; Chimborazo vs Everest (farthest from the centre).
- **z2-2 Otáčení Země** – rotation (24 h, west → east), day and night, the apparent motion of the Sun; local (solar) time; 15° = 1 hour; worked examples of local time differences.
- **z2-3 Časová pásma a datová hranice** – time zones, UTC, CET/CEST (letní čas); the date line and what happens when you cross it; flight-time puzzles as worked examples.
- **z2-4 Oběh Země kolem Slunce a roční období** – revolution (365¼ days, leap year); tilt of the axis 23,5°; solstices and equinoxes; why seasons are not caused by distance; polar day and night.
- **z2-5 Osvětlení Země a teplotní pásy** – angle of the Sun's rays; tropics and polar circles; tropical, temperate and polar zones (podnebné pásy as preview of level 4); length of day across latitudes.
- **z2-6 Měsíc a slapy** – Moon's motions and phases (recap from physics); tides (příliv a odliv) and why there are two a day; spring and neap tides; tidal energy and coasts.

### Level 3 – Reliéf Země
*Goal: explain how mountains, valleys and coasts form and why earthquakes and volcanoes happen where they do.*

- **z3-1 Stavba Země a litosférické desky** – crust, mantle, core (recap from biology, geography angle); continental vs oceanic crust; plates and their boundaries; moving continents (Pangea); the map of plates.
- **z3-2 Zemětřesení a sopky** – where and why (plate boundaries, hot spots); magnitude vs intensity in plain words; types of volcanoes; tsunami; living with risk (Japan, Iceland, Italy); risk = hazard × vulnerability (first idea).
- **z3-3 Jak vznikají pohoří** – vnitřní síly: folding (vrásnění) and faulting (zlomy, kry); young and old mountains (Himaláje vs Krkonoše); main mountain belts of the world; lowlands, uplands, plateaus (nížiny, vysočiny, plošiny).
- **z3-4 Zvětrávání a eroze** – vnější síly: mechanical and chemical weathering; erosion, transport, deposition; gravity (sesuvy); karst (kras: jeskyně, krápníky, propasti – Moravský kras).
- **z3-5 Práce řek, ledovců a větru** – river valleys (V-shaped), meanders, deltas; glacial landforms (U-shaped valleys, moraines, fjords); aeolian landforms (dunes); reading these forms on contour maps and photos.
- **z3-6 Pobřeží** – waves and coastal erosion (cliffs, beaches, spits); types of coasts (fjord, ria, lagoon, coral reef); human protection of coasts.
- **z3-7 Půdy a jejich ochrana** – soil as a sphere (pedosféra); soil profile and horizons (recap from biology); main soil types of the world and Czechia (černozem, hnědozem, podzol); erosion and soil protection.

### Level 4 – Podnebí, vody a krajinné pásy
*Goal: read weather and climate, explain where the world's climates, rivers and biomes are.*

- **z4-1 Atmosféra a počasí** – composition and layers of the atmosphere (troposphere, stratosphere, ozone); weather elements (temperature, pressure, humidity, clouds, precipitation, wind) and how they are measured; weather station; weather vs climate.
- **z4-2 Tlak, vítr a fronty** – high and low pressure; wind from high to low; cold and warm fronts; reading a synoptic map; weather forecast basics; storms and extremes (bouřky, tornáda, hurikány).
- **z4-3 Oběh vzduchu a podnebné pásy** – general circulation (Hadley cell in plain words, trade winds, westerlies); monsoons; climate zones and climate types (simplified Köppen); continental vs oceanic climate; altitude and climate.
- **z4-4 Klimatogram** – reading and drawing a climate chart (temperature line, precipitation bars); comparing places; worked examples (Praha, Manaus, Káhira, Jakutsk).
- **z4-5 Voda na Zemi** – the water cycle; salt vs fresh water shares; world ocean: salinity, currents (Golfský proud), waves; ocean currents and climate.
- **z4-6 Řeky, jezera a ledovce** – river parts, river basin (povodí), main watershed, drainage area (úmoří); discharge and regime; floods; lakes by origin; glaciers and permafrost; groundwater.
- **z4-7 Krajinné pásy a změna klimatu** – biomes (rainforest, savanna, desert, steppe, deciduous forest, taiga, tundra, polar) and their link to climate; altitudinal zonation; climate change as a first overview (greenhouse effect, warming, impacts) – deeper in level 10.

### Level 5 – Lidé na Zemi
*Goal: describe how many people live where, how populations change and move, and how places differ culturally and politically.*

- **z5-1 Kolik nás je a kde žijeme** – world population and its growth; uneven distribution; population density (worked examples); why people live where they do (physical and human factors); ecumene and anecumene.
- **z5-2 Porodnost, úmrtnost a věková pyramida** – birth and death rates, natural increase; life expectancy; population pyramids (young, ageing) and how to read them; first idea of the demographic transition.
- **z5-3 Migrace** – types (internal/international, voluntary/forced); push and pull factors; refugees; migration in Czech history and today.
- **z5-4 Jazyky, náboženství a kultury** – major language families and world languages; world religions and their regions; culture and identity; cultural diversity and conflicts; respect (avoid outdated „rasy").
- **z5-5 Sídla a města** – village vs town; functions of settlements; urbanisation and its stages; agglomeration, conurbation, megacity; problems of big cities; slums; suburbanisation.
- **z5-6 Státy a hranice** – state, nation, border; forms of government (monarchy, republic) and state structure (unitary, federal); dependent territories; international organisations (UN, EU, NATO) as a first overview.

### Level 6 – Hospodářství světa
*Goal: explain where and why things are grown, mined, made and traded, and how rich and poor countries differ.*

- **z6-1 Sektory hospodářství** – primary, secondary, tertiary (and quaternary) sectors; how the employment structure changes with development; GDP and GDP per capita (worked example).
- **z6-2 Zemědělství a výživa světa** – natural conditions for farming; crop and livestock regions; intensive vs extensive farming; hunger and food waste; fishing and forestry.
- **z6-3 Nerostné suroviny a energie** – fuels, ores, building materials; where they are; renewable vs non-renewable energy; energy mix of the world and Czechia; mining and the landscape.
- **z6-4 Průmysl** – location factors (raw materials, energy, labour, market, transport); traditional industry regions vs modern hi-tech; deindustrialisation; car industry as an example.
- **z6-5 Doprava a spoje** – transport types and their advantages; transport networks, hubs, canals (Panama, Suez); internet and data cables; transport and the environment.
- **z6-6 Služby, cestovní ruch a obchod** – services; tourism and its impacts; world trade, exports and imports; trade blocs (first idea).
- **z6-7 Globalizace a rozvoj** – globalisation and transnational companies; developed vs developing countries; indicators (GDP per capita, HDI, literacy, life expectancy); fair trade; how to read development maps.

### Level 7 – Regiony světa
*Goal: describe each world region with the tools of levels 1–6: nature, people, economy and their links.*

- **z7-1 Jak dělíme svět na regiony** – continents and oceans; world regions (by nature, culture, economy); mental maps; region as a geographer's tool.
- **z7-2 Afrika** – relief, climates and biomes (Sahara, Sahel, rainforest, savanna); population growth and urbanisation; colonial borders; resources and development; regions (North Africa, Sub-Saharan Africa).
- **z7-3 Asie: východ a jih** – monsoon Asia; China, Japan, Korea, India; population giants; industrial and technology hubs; rivers and rice.
- **z7-4 Asie: západ, střed a sever** – Middle East (oil, water, religions, conflicts); Central Asia; Siberia (Rusko in Asia); deserts and steppes.
- **z7-5 Severní Amerika** – relief (Rocky Mountains, Appalachians, Great Plains), climate and hazards (hurricanes, tornadoes); USA and Canada: population, cities, economy.
- **z7-6 Latinská Amerika** – Andes, Amazonia (the largest river basin, deforestation), Central America and the Caribbean; urbanisation and inequality; Spanish and Portuguese heritage.
- **z7-7 Austrálie a Oceánie** – the driest inhabited continent; Great Barrier Reef; Aboriginal peoples; New Zealand; Pacific islands and sea-level rise.
- **z7-8 Polární oblasti** – Arctic vs Antarctica (ocean vs continent); ice sheets; the Antarctic Treaty; Arctic shipping routes and resources; climate change at the poles.

### Level 8 – Evropa
*Goal: describe Europe's nature, people and integration, and its regions around Czechia.*

- **z8-1 Příroda Evropy** – position and borders of Europe; relief (Alps, Scandinavian mountains, North European Plain); climate (Gulf Stream, oceanic vs continental); rivers (Dunaj, Rýn, Volha) and seas.
- **z8-2 Obyvatelstvo a státy Evropy** – population, ageing and migration; languages and religions; states and their capitals; mental map of Europe.
- **z8-3 Evropská unie a integrace** – history and aims of European integration; EU institutions in plain words; single market, Schengen, the euro; Council of Europe vs EU; Czechia in the EU.
- **z8-4 Západní a severní Evropa** – United Kingdom, France, Benelux, the Nordic countries: nature, economy, cities.
- **z8-5 Jižní a jihovýchodní Evropa** – Mediterranean (Italy, Spain, Greece), Balkans: climate, tourism, history, migration routes.
- **z8-6 Střední a východní Evropa** – Germany, Poland, Austria, Slovakia, Hungary (Czechia's neighbours), Ukraine and Russia (European part); transformation after 1989; war in Ukraine as a geographical event.

### Level 9 – Česko
*Goal: describe Czechia's nature, people, economy and regions, and investigate one's own place.*

- **z9-1 Poloha a území Česka** – position in Europe; area and borders; historical lands (Čechy, Morava, Slezsko); development of the territory; Czechia as „střecha Evropy" (three seas).
- **z9-2 Příroda Česka** – Bohemian Massif and Carpathians; highest and lowest points (Sněžka 1 603 m, Labe at Hřensko); climate; rivers and watersheds; soils; national parks and CHKO.
- **z9-3 Obyvatelstvo a sídla Česka** – population number and structure (ČSÚ data); ageing; minorities and foreigners; settlement system; Prague and other cities; suburbanisation around Prague and Brno.
- **z9-4 Hospodářství Česka** – industry (automotive, engineering), agriculture, energy (coal, nuclear, renewables), transport (motorways, rail), services and tourism; regional differences.
- **z9-5 Kraje Česka** – the 14 regions: capitals and what each is known for; reading regional statistics; mental map of Czechia.
- **z9-6 Životní prostředí Česka** – air and water pollution, droughts and floods (2002, 2024), bark beetle and forests, protected areas; sustainable development locally.
- **z9-7 Moje obec: terénní výzkum** – planning a fieldwork investigation (question, hypothesis, data, map, conclusion); mapping one's surroundings; land use, traffic counts, interviews; presenting results; safety.

### Level 10 – Systémy Země a přírodní rizika
*Goal: explain Earth's physical systems quantitatively and assess natural hazards and climate change.*

- **z10-1 Krajinná sféra jako systém** – spheres and their interactions; inputs, outputs, stores and flows; feedback loops (positive, negative); the water cycle as a system with numbers.
- **z10-2 Energetická bilance a cirkulace atmosféry** – radiation budget; albedo; three-cell model (Hadley, Ferrel, polar); jet streams; ocean–atmosphere links (El Niño).
- **z10-3 Uhlíkový cyklus a klima** – carbon stores and flows (atmosphere, ocean, biosphere, rocks); how humans change them; CO₂ concentration over time (Mauna Loa).
- **z10-4 Změna klimatu: důkazy, příčiny, scénáře** – evidence (temperature records, ice cores, sea level, glaciers); natural vs human causes; scenarios (IPCC) in plain words; mitigation vs adaptation.
- **z10-5 Tektonická rizika** – plate tectonics in depth (subduction, hot spots); earthquakes and volcanoes as hazards; magnitude scales; prediction vs preparedness; case studies (Japan 2011, Iceland, Turkey–Syria 2023).
- **z10-6 Hydrometeorologická rizika** – tropical cyclones, floods (hydrograph reading), droughts, heatwaves; risk = hazard × vulnerability ÷ capacity; disaster risk management cycle.
- **z10-7 Krajiny ledovců, pouští a pobřeží** – glacial systems and retreat; hot deserts and desertification; coastal systems and sea-level rise; landscape as a system responding to change.
- **z10-8 Dálkový průzkum a GIS v praxi** – satellite data (Sentinel, Landsat); vegetation index in plain words; GIS analysis (overlay, buffer, heat map); a small project with open data.

### Level 11 – Obyvatelstvo, města a geopolitika
*Goal: explain population change, urban processes, cultural identity and the political organisation of the world.*

- **z11-1 Demografický přechod** – the demographic transition model, its stages and limits; population projections; ageing and shrinking populations; population policies (China, France).
- **z11-2 Migrace a teorie migrace** – migration flows in numbers; theories (push–pull, gravity model in plain words, Ravenstein's laws); remittances; brain drain; migration in Europe.
- **z11-3 Urbanizace a město 21. století** – urbanisation curve; urban models (concentric zones, sectors, multiple nuclei) and their limits; gentrification; smart and sustainable cities; informal settlements.
- **z11-4 Venkov a zemědělství** – rural change; agricultural systems; von Thünen in plain words; the Green Revolution; food security.
- **z11-5 Kultura a identita míst** – cultural diffusion; languages and religions spatially; place identity and globalisation (changing places); cultural landscapes and heritage (UNESCO, Czech sites).
- **z11-6 Státy, hranice a území** – sovereignty; types of borders; enclaves and microstates (Vatican, Monaco); disputed territories; separatism; state shapes and their consequences.
- **z11-7 Geopolitika a mezinárodní organizace** – power and space; UN, NATO, EU, G20, BRICS; alliances and blocs; global governance (climate, oceans, Antarctica).
- **z11-8 Konflikty ve světě** – causes of conflicts (resources, identity, borders); case studies (Middle East, Ukraine, Africa); refugees; peacekeeping.

### Level 12 – Globální hospodářství a udržitelnost
*Goal: explain the global economy, inequality and resource security, and evaluate paths to sustainable development.*

- **z12-1 Lokalizace hospodářských činností** – location factors and theories in plain words (Weber, clusters); global production networks; Czechia in supply chains.
- **z12-2 Globalizace a nadnárodní firmy** – flows of goods, capital, information and people; TNCs; global trade and its rules (WTO); winners and losers.
- **z12-3 Rozvoj a nerovnosti** – measuring development (GDP PPP, HDI, multidimensional poverty); core–periphery; theories of development (modernisation, dependency) in plain words; development aid; Gini in plain words.
- **z12-4 Voda jako zdroj** – water stress and scarcity; virtual water; conflicts over rivers; dams and their impacts; Czech drought and water management.
- **z12-5 Energetická bezpečnost** – energy sources and flows; energy mix and transition; dependence on imports; Europe after 2022; renewables and storage.
- **z12-6 Potraviny pro 10 miliard** – food production and distribution; land grabbing; diets and footprints; sustainable agriculture.
- **z12-7 Udržitelný rozvoj** – Agenda 2030 and the SDGs; ecological footprint; circular economy; planetary boundaries; what an individual and a municipality can do.
- **z12-8 Svět v roce 2050** – synthesis: population, climate, cities, resources, geopolitics; thinking in scenarios; how geographers work with uncertainty.

## Level tests

12 questions per level across all its lessons, mixed kinds (including map reading and graph reading), pass mark 70 %, reward: the level emblem.
