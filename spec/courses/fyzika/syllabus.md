# Fyzika – syllabus

From "what is a measurement" to the level expected at the Czech maturita and at university entrance (A-level / AP Physics). **12 levels, 80 lessons** (6–7 per level), plus a level test and mini-games in every level. Czech only, for learners 14–18 (the first levels also work for younger learners catching up).

**Mathematics.** Czech school physics avoids calculus; so does this course. Relations are built from graphs (slope = rate, area = accumulated quantity), proportionality and algebra. Vectors use components and right-angle trigonometry (taught in L8-1 where first needed); exponential decay is introduced by halving before the formula.

### Principles for the order

1. **Observe, then measure, then explain.** Every topic starts from an everyday observation, then a measurement, then a model (particles, forces, fields, waves, quanta).
2. **Spiral: ZŠ first, gymnázium again.** Each big area is met twice – qualitatively with simple formulas in levels 1–7, quantitatively in levels 8–12 (motion 2 → 8, fluids 3 → 9, heat 4 → 10, waves and sound 4/5 → 9, electricity 6/7 → 11, light 5 → 12, atom 7 → 12).
3. **Mechanics before everything.** Force, energy and momentum are the language the rest of physics is written in, so kinematics and dynamics come first in both halves.
4. **Energy as the thread.** Energy is introduced in L3 and every later level states what energy transfers happen (heat, sound, electricity, nuclear, light).
5. **Fields after forces, by analogy.** The gravitational field (L9) comes before the electric field (L11), so potential, field lines and energy in a field are learned once and reused.
6. **Oscillations before waves before light.** SHM (L9-5) → mechanical waves (L9-6) → EM oscillations and waves (L11-7) → wave optics (L12-1).
7. **Modern physics last**, when waves, energy, fields and the classical picture it breaks are all known.

## Detailed syllabus

Each lesson lists its **core content**. Content must cover every bullet and must not use concepts from later lessons without saying so. Worked examples accompany every formula.

### Level 1 – Měření a látky
*Goal: think like a physicist – measure carefully, use units, and picture matter as moving particles.*

- **f1-1 Co je fyzika a jak se měří** – what physics studies (tělesa, látky, jevy); physical quantity = number × unit; observation, experiment, hypothesis, variables (independent, dependent, controlled); a measurement protocol; lab safety for physics (electricity, heat, lasers).
- **f1-2 Veličiny, jednotky a převody** – SI base units (m, kg, s, A, K, mol, cd) and derived units; prefixes from nano to giga; converting units (length, area, volume, time, speed) step by step; scientific notation and orders of magnitude (from an atom to the Universe); estimating (Fermi questions).
- **f1-3 Měření délky, objemu a času** – measuring instruments and their resolution (pravítko, posuvné měřidlo, odměrný válec, stopky); reading scales and the meniscus; volume of irregular bodies by displacement; repeated measurement, average, spread and measurement error; significant figures (intro).
- **f1-4 Hmotnost a hustota** – mass vs weight (intuition, weight properly in f2-3); measuring mass (váhy); density ρ = m/V with unit conversions (kg/m³ ↔ g/cm³); table of densities; identifying a substance by its density; floating as a density comparison (explained properly in f3-2).
- **f1-5 Částicová stavba látek** – matter is made of particles in constant motion; evidence (diffusion, Brownian motion, dissolving, compressibility of gases); forces between particles; solids, liquids and gases in the particle model; changes of state as particle rearrangement (quantitative in f4-3).
- **f1-6 Teplota a teplotní roztažnost** – temperature as a measure of particle motion (intuition); thermometers and scales (°C, K, °F mention); absolute zero; thermal expansion of solids, liquids and gases, its uses and problems (bimetal, rail gaps, bridges); the anomaly of water.
- **f1-7 Elektrický náboj a magnety** – electrification by rubbing, two kinds of charge, attraction and repulsion, conductors and insulators, the electroscope, lightning (intro); permanent magnets, poles, magnetic field made visible with iron filings, Earth's magnetic field and the compass; magnetised and magnetic materials.

### Level 2 – Pohyb a síly
*Goal: describe motion, recognise forces and predict what they do.*

- **f2-1 Pohyb a jeho popis** – rest and motion are relative (frame of reference); trajectory, path length (dráha), displacement (intro); uniform and non-uniform motion; speed v = s/t, units km/h ↔ m/s; average speed; worked examples (school trip, runner, snail).
- **f2-2 Grafy pohybu** – distance–time graphs (reading, slope = speed); speed–time graphs (constant speed, speeding up, slowing down; area = distance); telling stories from graphs; acceleration as change of speed per second (intro).
- **f2-3 Síla a její měření** – force as an interaction (always between two bodies); effects of force (change of motion, deformation); measuring with a newton meter; weight F_G = m·g, g ≈ 9.81 N/kg (10 N/kg in estimates), mass on the Moon; representing forces with arrows; centre of mass (těžiště) and stability.
- **f2-4 Skládání sil a rovnováha** – resultant of forces along a line (same and opposite directions); forces at an angle (parallelogram, qualitative/graphical); equilibrium of forces; free-body diagrams for everyday situations (book on a table, hanging lamp, tug of war).
- **f2-5 Newtonovy pohybové zákony** – 1st law (inertia; seatbelts); 2nd law (F = m·a, qualitatively and with simple numbers); 3rd law (action and reaction act on different bodies; rockets, walking, swimming); common misconceptions (motion needs no force).
- **f2-6 Tření a odporové síly** – static and sliding friction, what it depends on (surface, normal force), F_t = f·F_N; rolling friction; air and water resistance, terminal speed; when friction helps (walking, brakes, tyres) and when it hurts; reducing friction (lubrication, bearings, streamlining).
- **f2-7 Otáčivé účinky síly: páka a kladka** – moment of a force M = F·d; the lever and its equilibrium condition; types of levers in tools and the body; pulleys (fixed, movable, block and tackle); the golden rule of mechanics (what you gain in force you lose in distance) – link to work in f3-4.

### Level 3 – Tlak a tekutiny, práce a energie
*Goal: understand pressure and buoyancy, and meet energy – the currency of physics.*

- **f3-1 Tlak a tlaková síla** – pressure p = F/S, unit pascal; pressure of solids (skis, knives, nails, tracks); pressure in liquids: Pascal's law, hydraulic press and brakes (F₁/S₁ = F₂/S₂); hydrostatic pressure p = h·ρ·g, water towers, dams, diving; communicating vessels.
- **f3-2 Vztlak a Archimédův zákon** – buoyant force F_vz = V·ρ_kap·g; Archimedes' principle with an experiment; sinking, floating, hovering compared by forces and by densities; ships, submarines, hydrometers, icebergs (why 90 % is under water); worked examples.
- **f3-3 Atmosférický tlak a plyny** – the atmosphere and its pressure (Torricelli, Magdeburg hemispheres); barometers and weather; pressure changes with altitude; overpressure and vacuum (tyres, suction cups, syringes); buoyancy in gases (balloons, airships).
- **f3-4 Práce a výkon** – mechanical work W = F·s (force along the motion), joule; when no work is done; power P = W/t, watt, horsepower (mention); efficiency η = useful/total; machines and the golden rule revisited (f2-7).
- **f3-5 Mechanická energie** – kinetic energy (E_k = ½mv²) and gravitational potential energy (E_p = m·g·h); energy transformations (pendulum, roller coaster, hydropower); conservation of energy and where "lost" energy goes (heat); worked problems (speed at the bottom of a slide).
- **f3-6 Zdroje energie kolem nás** – forms of energy (mechanical, internal, chemical, electrical, nuclear, radiant); energy chains (Sun → plant → food → muscles); energy of food and fuels (kJ); saving energy at home; preview of power plants (f7-5).

### Level 4 – Teplo a zvuk
*Goal: explain heating and cooling with energy and particles; understand sound as a wave.*

- **f4-1 Vnitřní energie a teplo** – internal energy of a body (particle motion and interactions); changing it by work (friction, compression) or by heating; heat Q as transferred energy; specific heat capacity, Q = m·c·Δt, water's high c (climate, cooling systems); calorimetry with mixing (simple heat balance).
- **f4-2 Šíření tepla** – conduction (conductors and insulators, why metal feels cold), convection (radiators, sea breeze, ocean currents), radiation (Sun, infrared, colour of surfaces, thermos flask); insulating houses (thermal imaging, U-value intuition).
- **f4-3 Změny skupenství** – melting/solidifying, evaporation/boiling, condensation, sublimation/deposition; melting and boiling points; latent heat (Q = m·l) and the heating curve; boiling point depends on pressure (pressure cooker, mountains); evaporation cools (sweat); humidity, dew, clouds.
- **f4-4 Tepelné motory** – turning heat into work; steam engine, petrol and diesel engines (four strokes), jet engine; efficiency of heat engines and why it is limited (intro to f10-5); environmental impact (CO₂, electric vehicles comparison); fridges and heat pumps (heat moved "uphill" using work).
- **f4-5 Zvuk a jeho šíření** – sources of sound (vibrations); sound needs a medium (vacuum bell); longitudinal wave (compressions and rarefactions); speed of sound in air, water and steel; reflection and echo, echolocation, sonar; calculating distance from an echo; thunder and lightning distance.
- **f4-6 Vlastnosti zvuku a hluk** – frequency and pitch, hertz, human hearing range, infrasound and ultrasound (medicine, animals); amplitude and loudness, decibels as a scale (qualitatively); timbre (tone vs noise, instruments); the ear; noise and hearing protection; resonance (glass, bridges – intro).

### Level 5 – Světlo
*Goal: follow light rays and explain images, colours and how we see.*

- **f5-1 Světlo a jeho šíření** – light sources (hot, luminescent), light travels in straight lines (rays and beams); speed of light c ≈ 3·10⁸ m/s, light-year; shadow and half-shadow; eclipses of the Sun and Moon; phases of the Moon; camera obscura.
- **f5-2 Odraz světla a zrcadla** – law of reflection; regular and diffuse reflection; plane mirror image (virtual, same size, reversed); concave and convex mirrors, focus, images (qualitative ray diagrams), uses (headlights, car mirrors, telescopes).
- **f5-3 Lom světla** – refraction at a boundary, towards/away from the normal; index of refraction n = c/v (intro); apparent depth (the "broken" straw, pool looks shallower); total internal reflection, critical angle, optical fibres and internet; mirages.
- **f5-4 Čočky a zobrazení** – converging and diverging lenses, focus, focal length, optical power (dioptre); the three special rays; real and virtual images; magnifying glass, projector, camera; image position qualitatively (lens equation in f12-2).
- **f5-5 Oko a optické přístroje** – structure of the eye and how it focuses (accommodation); short- and long-sightedness and their correction; the microscope and the telescope (how two lenses work together); protecting eyes (UV, lasers, screens).
- **f5-6 Barvy a spektrum** – dispersion by a prism, the rainbow; colours of objects (reflection/absorption); additive (RGB screens) and subtractive (CMY printing) colour mixing; infrared and ultraviolet beyond the visible; the electromagnetic spectrum (intro, detail in f11-7).

### Level 6 – Elektřina
*Goal: build and analyse circuits safely; understand current, voltage, resistance and power.*

- **f6-1 Elektrický náboj a elektrické pole** – charge carriers (electrons, ions), unit coulomb, elementary charge (intro); conductors and insulators at the particle level; electric field around charges, field lines (qualitative); electrostatic induction; uses and dangers (photocopier, sparks at petrol stations, lightning rod).
- **f6-2 Elektrický obvod, proud a napětí** – circuit elements and standard symbols; current I = Q/t, ammeter in series; voltage as energy per charge, voltmeter in parallel; sources (cells, batteries, mains); conventional current vs electron flow; short circuit.
- **f6-3 Elektrický odpor a Ohmův zákon** – Ohm's law I = U/R, measuring the I–U characteristic; resistance of a wire R = ρ·l/S; dependence on temperature; resistors, rheostats, potentiometers; non-ohmic components (filament lamp) – link to f6-6.
- **f6-4 Zapojení rezistorů** – series and parallel circuits: current, voltage and resistance rules, worked examples; why homes use parallel wiring; bulbs brightness puzzles; voltage divider (intro).
- **f6-5 Elektrická práce, výkon a bezpečnost** – electrical energy W = U·I·t, power P = U·I, kWh and the electricity bill; appliance labels; mains electricity (230 V, 50 Hz), fuse, circuit breaker, residual-current device (chránič), earthing, double insulation; electric shock and first aid.
- **f6-6 Polovodiče a elektronika** – semiconductors (silicon), how temperature and light change their resistance (thermistor, LDR); diode and LED (one-way valve, I–U graph); solar cells; sensors in phones; transistor as a switch (idea only); logic and digital information (intro).

### Level 7 – Magnetismus, energetika a vesmír
*Goal: connect electricity and magnetism, see where our electricity comes from, meet the atom's nucleus and our place in the Universe.*

- **f7-1 Magnetické pole elektrického proudu** – Oersted's experiment; field of a straight wire and of a coil (right-hand rule); electromagnets and their uses (cranes, relays, doorbells, MRI); magnetic materials (iron core).
- **f7-2 Elektromotor** – force on a current-carrying wire in a magnetic field (left-hand rule / Fleming, direction only); the DC motor (commutator); loudspeakers; motors around us.
- **f7-3 Elektromagnetická indukce** – Faraday's experiments; induced voltage when the magnetic field through a coil changes; what makes it bigger (speed, turns, stronger magnet); generators and dynamos; induction hob and wireless charging (idea).
- **f7-4 Střídavý proud a transformátor** – alternating current, frequency 50 Hz, its graph; transformer (U₁/U₂ = N₁/N₂) and why electricity is transmitted at high voltage (power losses); the grid from power plant to socket.
- **f7-5 Elektrárny a energetika** – thermal, nuclear, hydro, pumped storage, wind, solar, geothermal power plants and how each turns energy into electricity; efficiency, emissions and reliability; renewable vs non-renewable; the Czech energy mix; storing energy (batteries, pumped storage); saving energy.
- **f7-6 Atom, radioaktivita a jaderná energie** – atom and nucleus (protons, neutrons, electrons), isotopes; α, β, γ radiation, penetration and shielding, half-life (graphically), dose and protection; fission and chain reaction, nuclear power plant (Temelín, Dukovany), waste; fusion in the Sun.
- **f7-7 Sluneční soustava a vesmír** – the Sun, planets, moons, dwarf planets, asteroids and comets; day and night, seasons (axis tilt), phases of the Moon, tides (intro); stars and their life cycle (IGCSE), galaxies, the Milky Way; light-year and scale of the Universe; redshift and the expanding Universe (intro); space exploration.

### Level 8 – Kinematika a dynamika
*Goal: the quantitative mechanics toolkit of the gymnázium, A-level and AP.*

- **f8-1 Veličiny, vektory a nejistoty** – scalar and vector quantities; adding vectors (graphically, by components), resolving a vector with sin and cos (right-angle trigonometry recap); absolute and relative uncertainty, significant figures, error bars; plotting data and reading a straight-line fit (slope and intercept with units); dimensional analysis.
- **f8-2 Rovnoměrně zrychlený pohyb** – position, displacement, velocity, acceleration; uniformly accelerated motion: v = v₀ + a·t, s = v₀·t + ½·a·t², v² = v₀² + 2·a·s; s–t, v–t and a–t graphs (slope and area); braking distance and reaction time; worked multi-step problems.
- **f8-3 Volný pád a vrhy** – free fall (Galileo, g = 9.81 m/s²); vertical throw; independence of horizontal and vertical motion; horizontal projectile and oblique projectile (range, maximum height, time of flight, 45° without air resistance); air resistance qualitatively.
- **f8-4 Pohyb po kružnici** – period and frequency, angular velocity ω, v = ω·r; centripetal acceleration a = v²/r and the force that provides it (tension, friction, gravity, normal force); cars in curves, banking, loop-the-loop, washing machine; the "centrifugal force" misconception (inertial vs rotating frame).
- **f8-5 Newtonovy zákony v praxi** – inertial frames; F = m·a quantitatively; free-body diagrams with several forces; inclined plane (components of weight, normal force, friction); connected bodies (Atwood machine, block pulling block); lifts and apparent weight; terminal velocity.
- **f8-6 Hybnost a srážky** – momentum p = m·v; impulse F·Δt = Δp (airbags, crumple zones, catching a ball); conservation of momentum; elastic and inelastic collisions (1D, energy check); recoil and rockets; explosions.
- **f8-7 Práce, energie a výkon kvantitativně** – work W = F·s·cos α; work–energy theorem; conservation of mechanical energy with non-conservative forces (friction → heat); elastic potential energy ½k·x²; power P = F·v; efficiency chains; worked problems combining energy and momentum.

### Level 9 – Gravitace, rotace, kmity a vlny
*Goal: fields, rotation and periodic motion – the bridge from mechanics to waves.*

- **f9-1 Gravitační pole** – Newton's law of gravitation F = G·m₁m₂/r²; gravitational field strength and field lines; g at the Earth's surface and at height; gravitational potential energy in a field (−G·Mm/r, qualitatively and with the escape velocity); weightlessness in orbit.
- **f9-2 Keplerovy zákony a družice** – Kepler's three laws; circular orbits (v = √(G·M/r)), geostationary satellites, GPS; first and second cosmic velocities; planets and exoplanets; tides (quantitatively via the difference of attraction).
- **f9-3 Tuhé těleso a rovnováha** – rigid body, moment of a force (torque) M = F·d, couple; conditions of equilibrium (forces and moments); centre of gravity and stability; ladders, bridges, cranes, the human body (worked statics problems).
- **f9-4 Otáčivý pohyb** – rotational kinematics (angle, ω, angular acceleration); moment of inertia (how mass distribution matters); M = J·ε; rotational kinetic energy ½J·ω² (rolling down a slope); angular momentum and its conservation (figure skater, gyroscope, Earth).
- **f9-5 Mechanické kmitání** – periodic motion; simple harmonic motion (x = A·sin ωt, graphs of x, v, a), restoring force F = −k·x; spring oscillator T = 2π√(m/k), simple pendulum T = 2π√(l/g); energy in oscillations; damping; forced oscillations and resonance (bridges, buildings, musical instruments).
- **f9-6 Mechanické vlnění** – transverse and longitudinal waves; wavelength, frequency, speed v = λ·f; reflection and refraction of waves; superposition and interference (constructive/destructive, path difference); standing waves, nodes and antinodes; diffraction at a gap (Huygens principle, qualitatively).
- **f9-7 Akustika** – sound as a pressure wave; speed of sound and temperature; intensity and the decibel scale (L = 10·log(I/I₀)); standing waves in strings and pipes (harmonics, musical instruments); the Doppler effect (sirens, radar, medical ultrasound); ultrasound imaging.

### Level 10 – Molekulová fyzika a termodynamika
*Goal: explain heat and matter from the motion of particles; understand engines and the second law.*

- **f10-1 Kinetická teorie látek** – evidence for particles, amount of substance and the Avogadro constant (link to chemistry); particle speeds and their distribution; temperature as mean kinetic energy (E_k = 3/2·k·T); thermodynamic temperature, kelvin, absolute zero; thermal equilibrium (zeroth law).
- **f10-2 Vnitřní energie, teplo a kalorimetrie** – internal energy of an ideal gas; heat and work as two ways of changing it; specific and molar heat capacity; calorimetric equation with several bodies; latent heats; worked problems (mixing water and ice).
- **f10-3 Ideální plyn** – the ideal gas model; pressure from molecular collisions (qualitative derivation); equation of state p·V = n·R·T = N·k·T; isothermal, isobaric, isochoric and adiabatic processes with p–V graphs (Boyle, Charles, Gay-Lussac); worked problems (tyre pressure, diving, weather balloons).
- **f10-4 První termodynamický zákon** – ΔU = Q + W (sign convention stated clearly, compare with the A-level form); work done by a gas as the area under a p–V graph; applying the first law to each gas process; heating a gas at constant volume vs constant pressure.
- **f10-5 Tepelné stroje a druhý zákon** – cyclic processes; heat engine efficiency η = W/Q₁; the Carnot limit η = 1 − T₂/T₁; refrigerators and heat pumps (COP); the second law (heat flows from hot to cold, no perfect engine); entropy as spreading of energy (qualitative); real engines and power plants.
- **f10-6 Pevné látky a kapaliny** – crystalline and amorphous solids, crystal lattice; deformation, stress σ = F/S, strain ε, Hooke's law σ = E·ε, Young modulus, elastic limit and breaking (stress–strain curve); thermal expansion Δl = α·l₀·Δt; liquids: surface tension, capillarity, wetting.
- **f10-7 Změny skupenství a fázový diagram** – melting, boiling and sublimation at particle level; latent heat quantitatively; saturated vapour and its pressure; boiling vs evaporation; phase diagram of water (triple point, critical point); humidity, dew point and weather.

### Level 11 – Elektřina a magnetismus
*Goal: fields, circuits and electromagnetic induction at the level of maturita and A-level.*

- **f11-1 Elektrické pole** – Coulomb's law; electric field intensity E = F/Q, field lines of point charges and plates; potential and voltage (U = E·d in a uniform field); energy of a charge in a field (electronvolt); comparison with the gravitational field (f9-1); conductors and dielectrics in a field, Faraday cage.
- **f11-2 Kondenzátor a kapacita** – capacitance C = Q/U; parallel-plate capacitor C = ε·S/d, dielectric; energy stored ½C·U²; capacitors in series and parallel; charging and discharging (graphs, time constant R·C qualitatively); uses (camera flash, defibrillator, touchscreens).
- **f11-3 Obvody stejnosměrného proudu** – electromotive force and internal resistance (U = ε − R_i·I); Kirchhoff's laws; networks of resistors; potential divider with sensors; power transfer; measuring instruments and their effect on the circuit.
- **f11-4 Proud v polovodičích, kapalinách a plynech** – band idea of conductors, semiconductors and insulators; intrinsic and doped semiconductors (n, p); p–n junction and the diode, LED and solar cell; transistor (switch and amplifier idea); electrolysis and Faraday's law (link to chemistry); discharge in gases (spark, arc, neon, plasma, lightning).
- **f11-5 Magnetické pole a magnetické síly** – magnetic flux density B, tesla; force on a conductor F = B·I·l·sin α; Lorentz force on a moving charge F = B·Q·v; circular motion of charged particles (mass spectrometer, cyclotron, aurora); field of a wire and a solenoid; magnetic materials (dia-, para-, ferromagnetism).
- **f11-6 Elektromagnetická indukce** – magnetic flux Φ = B·S·cos α; Faraday's law U = −ΔΦ/Δt; Lenz's law and energy conservation; eddy currents (brakes, induction hobs); self-induction and inductance, energy in a coil.
- **f11-7 Střídavý proud a elektromagnetické vlny** – generation of AC, peak and effective (rms) values; resistance, inductive and capacitive reactance, RLC circuit and resonance (qualitatively plus X_L = ω·L, X_C = 1/(ω·C)); AC power; transformer quantitatively; LC oscillator and how an antenna radiates; electromagnetic waves, the full spectrum and uses (radio to gamma), c = λ·f.

### Level 12 – Optika a moderní fyzika
*Goal: light as a wave and a particle, relativity, the atom, the nucleus and the Universe.*

- **f12-1 Vlnová optika** – light as an EM wave; coherence; interference (Young's double slit, thin films – soap bubbles, anti-reflective coatings); diffraction at a slit and a grating (d·sin α = k·λ), spectrometers, CDs; polarisation (polaroid, LCD screens, sunglasses).
- **f12-2 Zobrazování zrcadly a čočkami** – sign convention; the imaging equation 1/a + 1/a′ = 1/f and magnification; ray diagrams for mirrors and lenses quantitatively; optical instruments (magnifier, microscope, telescope – magnification); lens aberrations; the eye as an optical system.
- **f12-3 Speciální teorie relativity** – Michelson–Morley and the constant speed of light; Einstein's postulates; relativity of simultaneity; time dilation (muons, GPS) and length contraction with the Lorentz factor; relativistic momentum (idea) and E = m·c²; mass–energy in nuclear reactions (link to f12-6).
- **f12-4 Fotony a vlnově-částicová dualita** – black-body radiation and Planck's quantum (E = h·f); the photoelectric effect and Einstein's equation (work function, threshold frequency, stopping voltage); photons in everyday tech (solar cells, cameras); de Broglie wavelength, electron diffraction; the uncertainty principle (qualitatively).
- **f12-5 Atom a jeho spektra** – Rutherford's experiment and model; Bohr model of hydrogen and energy levels (E_n = −13.6 eV/n²); emission and absorption spectra, spectral analysis (stars, flame tests); quantum numbers and the electron cloud (link to chemistry); laser (stimulated emission) and its uses.
- **f12-6 Atomové jádro a radioaktivita** – nucleus, nuclides, strong force; mass defect and binding energy per nucleon (the curve, why fission and fusion release energy); radioactive decay law N = N₀·(1/2)^(t/T), activity in becquerels; α, β⁻, β⁺, γ decay equations; fission reactors, fusion (ITER, the Sun); dosimetry and radiation in medicine.
- **f12-7 Částice a vesmír** – the standard model: quarks, leptons, force carriers, antiparticles, conservation laws in particle reactions; accelerators and detectors (CERN); stars: luminosity, HR diagram, stellar evolution, white dwarfs, neutron stars, black holes; Hubble's law, the Big Bang, cosmic microwave background, dark matter and dark energy (what we know and don't).

## Level tests

Each level ends with **Závěrečná výzva** (12 questions across the level, mixed kinds). Passing (≥ 70 %) awards the level badge and adds the level's emblem (unit or constant) to the learner's *Sbírka jednotek a konstant*.

## Coordination with the chemistry course

Topics that also appear in chemistry are taught from the physics angle and may point to the chemistry lesson: particles and states (f1-5 ↔ l1-2), atom and radioactivity (f7-6, f12-5, f12-6 ↔ l2-2, l2-7), amount of substance (f10-1 ↔ l4-4), electrolysis (f11-4 ↔ l6-7), energy and the climate (f7-5 ↔ l9-8). Never duplicate a chemistry lesson word for word.
