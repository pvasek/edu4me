# Chemie – syllabus

From "what is a substance" to the level expected at the Czech maturita and at university entrance (A-level / AP Chemistry). **9 levels, 63 lessons** (6–8 per level), plus a level test and mini-games in every level.

## Sources and how the order was chosen

The order follows the Czech curriculum and was cross-checked against the two big international tracks:

| Curriculum | What it covers | How it is used here |
|---|---|---|
| **RVP ZV** (Rámcový vzdělávací program pro základní vzdělávání), vzdělávací obor *Chemie*, 8.–9. ročník | Pozorování a pokus, bezpečnost, směsi, částicové složení látek a chemické prvky, chemické reakce, anorganické sloučeniny, organické sloučeniny, chemie a společnost | Levels 1–5 cover the whole ZŠ scope, in its order (látky a směsi → částice → prvky a vazby → reakce → oxidy, kyseliny, hydroxidy, soli). |
| **RVP G** (gymnázium), *Chemie*: obecná, anorganická, organická chemie, biochemie | Stavba atomu (orbitaly), periodický zákon, chemická vazba, chemické výpočty, termochemie, kinetika, rovnováha, redox, prvky s- p- d-bloku, uhlovodíky, deriváty, přírodní látky | Levels 2–9. The usual gymnázium sequence is obecná → anorganická → organická → biochemie; we keep it. |
| **Cambridge IGCSE Chemistry (0620)** | States of matter, atoms/elements/compounds, stoichiometry, electrochemistry, energetics, rates, equilibria, acids/bases/salts, Periodic Table, metals, environment, organic chemistry, experimental techniques | Cross-check that nothing a 16-year-old abroad learns is missing; used for the practical/separation topics and the environment lesson. |
| **A-level Chemistry** (AQA/OCR) and **AP Chemistry** (9 units) | Atomic structure, amount of substance, bonding, energetics, kinetics, equilibria and Kc, redox and electrode potentials, periodicity, group 2 and 7, transition metals, organic mechanisms, polymers, amino acids and DNA | Sets the top of the course ("pre-university"). Topics such as Kc, Ka, pH of weak acids, buffers, Hess's law, reaction mechanisms and electrode potentials come from here. |

Principles for the order:
1. **Macro before micro.** Learners first observe substances (level 1), then explain them with particles (level 2).
2. **Atoms before bonds, bonds before names.** Nomenclature (level 3) needs oxidation numbers, which need electronegativity and bonding.
3. **Equations before moles, moles before calculations.** Level 4 builds the whole quantitative toolkit that levels 5–9 use.
4. **Acids and bases twice.** Descriptive (Arrhenius, pH, salts) in level 5, quantitative (Ka, buffers) in level 6, as in the ZŠ → gymnázium spiral.
5. **General chemistry before descriptive chemistry.** Redox, energetics and equilibrium (level 6) come before the chemistry of the elements (level 7), which then uses them to explain *why* elements behave as they do.
6. **Organic before biochemistry.** Biomolecules are explained through their functional groups.

## Revision 2 – curriculum audit (what changed and why)

The first version squeezed several A-level/AP and RVP G topics into one lesson or left them out. The audit compared every lesson with RVP ZV/G, IGCSE 0620 and A-level/AP and changed:

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **New l2-7 Radioaktivita a jaderné reakce** (split from l2-3) | RVP G lists radioactivity and nuclear reactions; one bullet inside the isotope lesson was too little. Nuclear equations, fission/fusion, dose and safety were missing. |
| **New l3-7 Tvary molekul: VSEPR, hybridizace a polarita** (split from l3-2) | Hybridisation (sp, sp², sp³) is standard in Czech gymnázium *obecná chemie* and was missing; VSEPR with 5 and 6 electron domains, resonance and formal charge are A-level/AP. Lewis structures come first, shapes second. |
| **l6-2 split into l6-2 Galvanické články and new l6-7 Elektrolýza a Faradayovy zákony** | Electrolysis products in aqueous solution, chlor-alkali and Faraday's laws (Q = I·t) were missing; one lesson carried two big topics. |
| **New l6-8 Entropie a Gibbsova energie** | Spontaneity (ΔG = ΔH − TΔS) is AP unit 9 and A-level; was only "optional outlook". |
| **l6-4 extended with rate equations, orders and half-life; Kc moved into l6-5** | Rate laws and orders from initial-rate data are A-level/AP core. Kc belongs with equilibrium; l6-6 now focuses on weak acids, buffers and Ksp. |
| **New l7-7 Důkazy iontů a plynů** | IGCSE "Chemical analysis" (tests for cations, anions, gases, water) was scattered or missing. |
| **l8-6 split: Dusíkaté deriváty a mechanismy + new l8-7 Polymery a plasty** | Polymers deserved their own lesson (addition vs condensation, repeat units, recycling). |
| **New l8-8 Jak určit strukturu: spektroskopie a chromatografie** | Mass spectrometry, IR and ¹H NMR are A-level/AP and were missing entirely. |
| **New l9-7 Metabolismus: ATP, dýchání a fotosyntéza** | RVP G biochemistry includes metabolism (ATP, glycolysis, Krebs cycle, respiratory chain, photosynthesis phases). |
| **l9-6 split: Léčiva a chemie potravin + new l9-8 Energie, klima a zelená chemie** | One lesson carried drugs, food, fuels, climate and green chemistry. |
| Extra depth in existing lessons (see bullets marked *(rev 2)*) | Colloids and the Tyndall effect, the scientific method, successive ionisation energies, formal charge and resonance, calorimetry (q = m·c·ΔT), ICE tables, Q vs K, pH calculations, titration calculations, atom economy, coordination-compound naming, SN1/SN2, E/Z isomers, carbon hybridisation. |

Order checks that were kept on purpose:
- **Ions are named in l2-1** before electrons are explained (l2-2, l2-4); l2-1 only uses the names, the "why" comes in l2-4.
- **pH in l5-3 uses Kw** before equilibrium (level 6); it is given as a fact and explained in l6-5/l6-6 (spiral).
- **Haber–Bosch and the contact process appear in l6-5** as equilibrium case studies before the descriptive chemistry of level 7, which refers back to them.
- **Types of reactions (l4-3) mention neutralisation and precipitation** before level 5; only as recognisable patterns.

## Detailed syllabus

Each lesson lists its **core content**. Content must cover every bullet and must not use concepts from later lessons without saying so. Lessons are listed in the order the learner meets them (ids are stable identifiers, not positions).

### Level 1 – Svět látek
*Goal: see the world as made of substances, work safely, handle mixtures and solutions.*

- **l1-1 Co je chemie a jak bezpečně pracovat** – what chemistry studies (substances and their changes); physical vs chemical change (first intuition); chemistry around us; how scientists work: observation vs experiment, hypothesis, variables, a simple lab protocol *(rev 2)*; lab equipment; safety rules; GHS hazard pictograms; first aid basics; how to heat a test tube.
- **l1-2 Vlastnosti látek a skupenství** – substance vs body (látka × těleso); properties: colour, smell, density (ρ = m/V, simple calculation), melting and boiling point, solubility, electrical and thermal conductivity; states of matter and changes of state (tání, tuhnutí, vypařování, var, kapalnění, sublimace, desublimace); heating curve with plateaus *(rev 2)*; particle view of states; diffusion and Brownian motion.
- **l1-3 Směsi a chemicky čisté látky** – pure substance vs mixture; homogeneous vs heterogeneous mixtures; suspension, emulsion, foam, aerosol, smoke, fog; **colloids and the Tyndall effect** *(rev 2)*; alloys; examples from home; elements vs compounds as pure substances (intuition, no atoms yet); how to tell a pure substance: sharp melting/boiling point *(rev 2)*.
- **l1-4 Jak oddělit složky směsí** – usazování a dekantace, filtrace, odpařování a krystalizace, destilace (simple and fractional), sublimace, chromatografie (paper chromatography, Rf value *(rev 2)*), extraction *(rev 2)*, magnetic separation, centrifuge; choosing the right method; water treatment as an application.
- **l1-5 Roztoky a hmotnostní zlomek** – solution = solute + solvent; saturated, unsaturated, supersaturated; solubility and temperature (solubility curve reading, crystallisation on cooling); factors speeding up dissolving; **mass fraction** w = m(látky)/m(roztoku), percentages, mixing and diluting calculations (the "křížové pravidlo" as a tip).
- **l1-6 Voda a vzduch** – types of water (destilovaná, pitná, užitková, odpadní, minerální, tvrdá/měkká); water treatment plant and wastewater treatment plant (úpravna × čistička); composition of air (N₂ 78 %, O₂ 21 %, Ar ~0,9 %, CO₂ ~0,04 %); fractional distillation of liquid air *(rev 2)*; oxygen and combustion, fire triangle, extinguishing; air pollution (smog, ozone layer vs ground-level ozone, acid rain intro).

### Level 2 – Uvnitř atomu
*Goal: explain matter with particles; read the periodic table like a map.*

- **l2-1 Atomy, molekuly a ionty** – particle model; atom, molecule, ion (cation, anion) as names; chemical element vs compound at particle level; chemical symbols and simple formulas (H₂, O₂, H₂O, CO₂, NaCl); index vs coefficient; reading a formula (how many atoms of each element) *(rev 2)*; brief history (Démokritos → Dalton, Dalton's atomic theory postulates *(rev 2)*).
- **l2-2 Stavba atomu: jádro a obal** – nucleus (protons, neutrons) and electron cloud; charges and masses of particles (table with relative and absolute values *(rev 2)*); size of the nucleus vs the atom (stadium analogy); Thomson → Rutherford (gold foil: what was observed and what it proved *(rev 2)*) → Bohr → quantum model; atom is neutral: p⁺ = e⁻; strong nuclear force keeps the nucleus together *(rev 2)*.
- **l2-3 Protonové číslo, nukleonové číslo a izotopy** – Z, A, N = A − Z; nuclide notation; isotopes (hydrogen: protium, deuterium, tritium; carbon-12/13/14); relative atomic mass as a weighted average (calculations with Cl, Cu, B); atomic mass unit u; how the mass spectrometer measures isotope abundance (idea only) *(rev 2)*; ions: counting protons, neutrons and electrons in ions.
- **l2-7 Radioaktivita a jaderné reakce** *(new, rev 2)* – unstable nuclei; α, β⁻, β⁺ and γ radiation: nature, charge, penetration and shielding; writing and balancing nuclear equations (conservation of A and Z); decay series (idea); half-life and calculations (after n half-lives: m = m₀/2ⁿ); radiocarbon dating; fission (U-235, chain reaction, nuclear power plant, Temelín/Dukovany) and fusion (Sun, ITER); uses in medicine (PET, radiotherapy, iodine-131) and industry; dose, sievert, background radiation, protection; Becquerel, Marie Curie-Skłodowská.
- **l2-4 Elektronové vrstvy a valenční elektrony** – shells (K, L, M, N) and the 2-8-8 model for Z ≤ 20; valence electrons; octet/stable noble-gas configuration; why atoms form ions (Na → Na⁺, Cl → Cl⁻); first ionisation energy and **successive ionisation energies as evidence for shells** *(rev 2)*; electron-dot (Lewis) symbols.
- **l2-5 Orbitaly a elektronová konfigurace** – orbital as probability region; quantum numbers n, l, m, s (overview with the allowed values and the number of orbitals per subshell *(rev 2)*); s, p, d, f orbital shapes; Aufbau principle (diagonal rule), Pauli principle, Hund's rule; box diagrams; writing configurations incl. noble-gas shorthand; exceptions Cr, Cu; configurations of ions (4s electrons leave first *(rev 2)*); link between configuration and position in the table.
- **l2-6 Periodická soustava prvků** – Mendělejev and the periodic law (predicting gallium/germanium *(rev 2)*); groups and periods; s-, p-, d-, f-blocks; group names; metals, nonmetals, metalloids; trends: atomic radius, ionic radius *(rev 2)*, ionisation energy, electron affinity *(rev 2)*, electronegativity, metallic character – each explained by nuclear charge, shielding and distance; predicting properties from position.

### Level 3 – Vazby a názvosloví
*Goal: explain why atoms bond and name/write inorganic binary compounds.*

- **l3-1 Proč se atomy spojují: elektronegativita** – energy decreases when a bond forms (energy vs distance curve *(rev 2)*); bond energy and bond length (single > double > triple length *(rev 2)*); electronegativity (Pauling scale), trend in the table; ΔX and bond type (nonpolar < 0,4 ≤ polar < 1,7 ≤ ionic), the continuum between covalent and ionic; examples.
- **l3-2 Kovalentní vazba a Lewisovy vzorce** – shared electron pair; single, double, triple bond; σ and π bonds; **step-by-step method for Lewis structures** *(rev 2)*; lone pairs; coordinate (dative) bond (NH₄⁺, H₃O⁺, CO); **formal charge** and **resonance** (O₃, NO₃⁻, CO₃²⁻) *(rev 2)*; exceptions to the octet (BF₃, PCl₅, SF₆) *(rev 2)*.
- **l3-7 Tvary molekul: VSEPR, hybridizace a polarita** *(new, rev 2)* – VSEPR: electron domains, bonding and lone pairs, lone pairs repel more; shapes for 2–6 domains (lineární, trojúhelníková, tetraedrická, trigonální pyramida, lomená, trigonální bipyramida, oktaedrická, čtvercová) with angles (BeCl₂, BF₃, CH₄, NH₃, H₂O, PCl₅, SF₆, XeF₄); **hybridisation** sp³, sp², sp (and sp³d, sp³d² as a mention) linked to shapes and to σ/π bonds (ethane, ethene, ethyne); polar bonds vs polar molecules, dipole moment (CO₂ vs H₂O, CCl₄ vs CH₂Cl₂).
- **l3-3 Iontová a kovová vazba** – ionic bond as electrostatic attraction; ionic lattice (NaCl); lattice energy grows with charge and shrinks with size (qualitative) *(rev 2)*; properties of ionic compounds; metallic bond (electron sea) and properties of metals, alloys *(rev 2)*; molecular vs giant covalent structures (diamond, graphite, SiO₂); comparison table of the four structure types *(rev 2)*.
- **l3-4 Mezimolekulové síly a vlastnosti látek** – London (dispersion), dipole–dipole forces; hydrogen bonds; which force dominates and how it sets boiling points (alkanes, hydrides of groups 14–17) *(rev 2)*; why ice floats; solubility "like dissolves like"; surface tension and viscosity.
- **l3-5 Oxidační číslo a chemické vzorce** – definition of oxidation number; rules; calculating oxidation numbers in compounds and ions; the Czech endings I–VIII; writing a formula from a name via the "cross rule" and back.
- **l3-6 Názvosloví dvouprvkových sloučenin** – oxidy, peroxidy, halogenidy, sulfidy, hydridy, nitridy, karbidy; formula ↔ name in both directions; elements with several oxidation numbers (FeO × Fe₂O₃, CuCl × CuCl₂) *(rev 2)*; important trivial names; common real-world compounds; names of simple cations and anions (amonný kation, hydroxidový anion, kyanidový anion) *(rev 2)*.

### Level 4 – Reakce a výpočty
*Goal: write and balance equations, master the mole and chemical calculations.*

- **l4-1 Chemická reakce a zákon zachování hmotnosti** – reactants and products; signs of a reaction; the law of conservation of mass (Lomonosov, Lavoisier) and the law of definite proportions *(rev 2)*; chemical equation meaning; states (s), (l), (g), (aq); ionic and short ionic equations (intro), spectator ions *(rev 2)*.
- **l4-2 Vyčíslování chemických rovnic** – coefficients vs indices; step-by-step method; balancing combustion (incl. fractional coefficients doubled *(rev 2)*); balancing ionic equations (charge balance) *(rev 2)*; balancing by oxidation numbers is in l6-1.
- **l4-3 Typy chemických reakcí** – syntéza, rozklad, substituce, podvojná záměna (incl. precipitation, neutralisation, gas-forming); exothermic vs endothermic (intro); fast/slow; reversible reactions (intro, ⇌); redox and acid-base as families; reactivity series of metals as a prediction tool (intro) *(rev 2)*.
- **l4-4 Látkové množství a molární hmotnost** – the mole, N_A; n = N/N_A; molar mass M, n = m/M; molar volume (22,4 dm³/mol at STP, 24 dm³/mol at room conditions *(rev 2)*); ideal gas equation pV = nRT with unit conversions *(rev 2)*; mass percentage of an element; empirical and molecular formula from composition.
- **l4-5 Koncentrace roztoků a ředění** – molar concentration c = n/V; preparing a solution of given concentration (standard solution, volumetric flask *(rev 2)*); conversion between w and c using density; dilution c₁V₁ = c₂V₂; mixing solutions; mass concentration (g/dm³) *(rev 2)*.
- **l4-6 Výpočty z chemických rovnic** – mole ratio; mass → moles → moles → mass; gas volumes; limiting reagent and excess; percentage yield; **atom economy** *(rev 2)*; purity of a sample *(rev 2)*; worked multi-step problems.

### Level 5 – Kyseliny, zásady a soli
*Goal: recognise, name and use acids, bases and salts; understand pH.*

- **l5-1 Kyseliny a jejich názvosloví** – Arrhenius acid; binary acids and oxoacids; naming oxoacids from the oxidation number; polyprotic acids *(rev 2)*; deriving formulas; typical reactions of acids (with metals, oxides, carbonates) *(rev 2)*; important acids and their uses; safety; acid rain.
- **l5-2 Hydroxidy a zásady** – hydroxides and naming; alkalis vs insoluble hydroxides *(rev 2)*; ammonia as a base; basic and amphoteric oxides (CaO + H₂O, Al(OH)₃) *(rev 2)*; typical reactions of bases (with acids, acidic oxides, ammonium salts) *(rev 2)*; uses; safety; worked examples *(rev 2)*.
- **l5-3 pH a indikátory** – autoionisation of water, Kw = 10⁻¹⁴ (given, explained in level 6); pH = −log[H₃O⁺], pOH, pH + pOH = 14; **calculations for strong acids and bases incl. dilution and diprotic acids** *(rev 2)*; pH scale with everyday examples; indicators and their colour ranges; red cabbage.
- **l5-4 Neutralizace a titrace** – neutralisation; ionic equation; heat of neutralisation; acid–base titration procedure (standard solution, burette, pipette, indicator choice, equivalence vs end point) *(rev 2)*; **titration calculations step by step (1:1 and 1:2 ratios)** *(rev 2)*; titration curves (strong–strong, weak–strong); antacids, soil liming.
- **l5-5 Soli a jejich názvosloví** – ways to prepare salts; naming salts of oxoacids; hydrogen salts, hydrates; solubility rules and precipitation (net ionic equations) *(rev 2)*; important salts in everyday life.
- **l5-6 Teorie kyselin a zásad podle Brønsteda** – proton donor/acceptor; conjugate acid–base pairs; amphoteric substances; strong vs weak acids and bases, degree of dissociation; hydrolysis of salts (qualitative); Lewis acids and bases (with BF₃ + NH₃ and metal ions) *(rev 2)*.

### Level 6 – Energie, rychlost a rovnováha
*Goal: the physical chemistry toolkit — redox, electrochemistry, energy, rates and equilibria.*

- **l6-1 Redoxní reakce** – oxidation/reduction as electron transfer and change of oxidation number; oxidising and reducing agents; half-equations; balancing by oxidation numbers **and by the ion-electron method in acidic solution** (KMnO₄, K₂Cr₂O₇) *(rev 2)*; disproportionation *(rev 2)*; redox titration (manganometrie) as an application *(rev 2)*; redox around us.
- **l6-2 Galvanické články a elektrodové potenciály** – reactivity series (Beketov) and displacement; galvanic cell (Daniell), anode/cathode, salt bridge, cell notation Zn|Zn²⁺‖Cu²⁺|Cu *(rev 2)*; standard hydrogen electrode, standard electrode potential E°, E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode, predicting feasibility *(rev 2)*; batteries and accumulators (Li-ion, lead-acid), fuel cell; corrosion and protection (sacrificial anode, galvanising).
- **l6-7 Elektrolýza a Faradayovy zákony** *(new, rev 2, split from l6-2)* – electrolysis vs galvanic cell; electrolysis of molten salts; aqueous solutions: which ion is discharged at each electrode (H₂ vs metal, O₂ vs halogen); electrolysis of brine (chlor-alkali: Cl₂, H₂, NaOH); electroplating and copper refining; aluminium production (overview); **Faraday's laws**: Q = I·t, n(e⁻) = Q/F, m = M·I·t/(z·F), worked calculations.
- **l6-3 Termochemie a Hessův zákon** – system and surroundings; exothermic and endothermic; ΔH and thermochemical equations; **calorimetry: q = m·c·ΔT and ΔH from an experiment** *(rev 2)*; standard enthalpies of formation and combustion; Hess's law and calculations (cycle and ΔH = ΣΔfH(products) − ΣΔfH(reactants)); bond enthalpies (average values, why estimates differ).
- **l6-8 Entropie a Gibbsova energie** *(new, rev 2)* – spontaneous vs non-spontaneous processes; entropy as dispersal of energy/particles, predicting the sign of ΔS; ΔS° from tabulated S°; second law; Gibbs energy ΔG = ΔH − TΔS; the four sign combinations and the temperature where a reaction becomes feasible; ΔG and K (ΔG° = −RT ln K, qualitative), ΔG = −zFE (link to l6-2); kinetic vs thermodynamic stability (diamond → graphite).
- **l6-4 Rychlost reakce, rychlostní rovnice a katalýza** – rate definition and measuring it; collision theory, activation energy, Maxwell–Boltzmann distribution; factors: concentration, temperature (van 't Hoff rule of thumb), surface area, catalyst; enzymes and catalytic converters; **rate equation v = k·[A]^m·[B]^n, orders from initial-rate data, units of k, half-life of a first-order reaction, rate-determining step and mechanism, Arrhenius equation (qualitative)** *(rev 2)*.
- **l6-5 Chemická rovnováha, Le Chatelier a Kc** – reversible reactions and dynamic equilibrium; Le Chatelier's principle (concentration, pressure, temperature, catalyst: none); Haber–Bosch and contact process as case studies; **Kc expression (heterogeneous equilibria: no solids/pure liquids), meaning of its size, ICE-table calculations, reaction quotient Q vs K, Kp as a mention, only temperature changes K** *(rev 2, moved from l6-6)*; everyday examples.
- **l6-6 Slabé kyseliny, pufry a součin rozpustnosti** – Ka, pKa, Kb, pKa + pKb = 14 *(rev 2)*; pH of weak acids (approximation and when it holds) and weak bases *(rev 2)*; buffers: composition, how they work, Henderson–Hasselbalch, blood buffer; **solubility product Ks: expression, solubility from Ks, common-ion effect, will a precipitate form (Q vs Ks)** *(rev 2)*.

### Level 7 – Chemie prvků
*Goal: descriptive inorganic chemistry, explained with levels 2–6.*

- **l7-1 Vodík, kyslík a voda** – hydrogen: properties, isotopes, preparation (Zn + HCl, electrolysis, steam reforming *(rev 2)*), uses, hydrogen as fuel, hydrides (ionic vs covalent) *(rev 2)*; oxygen and ozone, preparation, oxides (acidic, basic, amphoteric, neutral); water: structure, anomalies, hardness; hydrogen peroxide (oxidant and reductant) *(rev 2)*.
- **l7-2 Halogeny a vzácné plyny** – group 17 trends; displacement reactions (with half-equations) *(rev 2)*; chlorine (preparation, disinfection, bleach, disproportionation in NaOH *(rev 2)*), hydrogen halides and their acidity, halides and the AgNO₃ test; fluorine, iodine; noble gases.
- **l7-3 Síra, dusík a fosfor** – sulfur: allotropes, H₂S, SO₂, H₂SO₄ (contact process, dehydrating and oxidising effect of concentrated acid *(rev 2)*), sulfates; nitrogen: N₂ inertness, NH₃, ammonium salts, HNO₃ (Ostwald, oxidising acid) *(rev 2)*, nitrogen oxides and smog, nitrogen cycle and fertilisers; phosphorus: white/red, phosphoric acid, phosphates, eutrophication.
- **l7-4 Uhlík a křemík** – carbon allotropes; CO and CO₂; carbonic acid, carbonates and hydrogen carbonates, limestone cycle, karst; silicon: semiconductors, SiO₂, silicates, glass, ceramics, cement; group 14 trend from nonmetal to metal (Sn, Pb) *(rev 2)*.
- **l7-5 Alkalické kovy a kovy alkalických zemin** – group 1 and 2 trends with explanations *(rev 2)*; reactions with water and oxygen (equations) *(rev 2)*; flame tests; important compounds; thermal decomposition of carbonates down group 2 *(rev 2)*; water hardness and its removal; magnesium and calcium in the body.
- **l7-6 Hliník, železo, měď a přechodné kovy** – aluminium; metallurgy: ores, reduction methods (C, CO, electrolysis) chosen by reactivity *(rev 2)*, blast furnace, steel, rusting; copper and alloys; zinc, chromium, silver, gold, platinum; properties of transition metals (variable oxidation numbers, coloured compounds and why *(rev 2)*, catalysts); coordination compounds: ligand, central atom, coordination number, **Czech naming of simple complexes** *(rev 2)*.
- **l7-7 Důkazy iontů a plynů** *(new, rev 2)* – why chemists test; flame tests (recap); cations with NaOH(aq) and NH₃(aq): Cu²⁺ blue, Fe²⁺ green, Fe³⁺ red-brown, Al³⁺ and Zn²⁺ white (dissolves in excess), Ca²⁺, NH₄⁺ (NH₃ turns litmus blue); anions: CO₃²⁻ (acid → CO₂ → limewater), SO₄²⁻ (BaCl₂), Cl⁻/Br⁻/I⁻ (AgNO₃ colours and NH₃ solubility), NO₃⁻ (Devarda/Al + NaOH → NH₃); gases: H₂ (squeaky pop), O₂ (glowing splint), CO₂ (limewater), Cl₂ (bleaches damp litmus), NH₃; water: anhydrous CuSO₄ / CoCl₂ paper, purity by boiling point; planning an identification (flowchart); instrumental methods as an outlook (links to l8-8).

### Level 8 – Organická chemie
*Goal: read, name and predict the behaviour of organic molecules.*

- **l8-1 Uhlík v organice: vzorce a izomerie** – why carbon is special; **hybridisation of carbon sp³/sp²/sp and bond angles** *(rev 2)*; types of formulas; classification of carbon chains; isomerism: constitutional (chain, position, functional), stereoisomerism (cis/trans and **E/Z** *(rev 2)*, optical isomerism, chiral centre, enantiomers and their different biological effects *(rev 2)*).
- **l8-2 Alkany a cykloalkany** – homologous series; names methane–dekan; IUPAC naming of branched alkanes; properties and boiling-point trend; combustion (complete/incomplete, CO danger); radical substitution with the three steps (initiation, propagation, termination) *(rev 2)*; crude oil, fractional distillation, cracking *(rev 2)*, octane number; cycloalkanes and conformations (brief).
- **l8-3 Alkeny, alkyny a aromáty** – double and triple bond, naming; electrophilic addition with mechanism and carbocation stability *(rev 2)*, Markovnikov's rule; bromine water test; polymerisation (pointer to l8-7); benzene: structure (Kekulé vs delocalised), aromaticity, electrophilic substitution (nitration, halogenation, Friedel–Crafts as a mention *(rev 2)*), important arenes, toxicity.
