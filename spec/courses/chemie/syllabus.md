# Chemie – syllabus

From "what is a substance" to the level expected at the Czech maturita and at university entrance (A-level / AP Chemistry). **9 levels × 6 lessons = 54 lessons**, plus a level test and mini-games in every level.

## Sources and how the order was chosen

The order follows the Czech curriculum and was cross-checked against the two big international tracks:

| Curriculum | What it covers | How it is used here |
|---|---|---|
| **RVP ZV** (Rámcový vzdělávací program pro základní vzdělávání), vzdělávací obor *Chemie*, 8.–9. ročník | Pozorování a pokus, bezpečnost, směsi, částicové složení látek a chemické prvky, chemické reakce, anorganické sloučeniny, organické sloučeniny, chemie a společnost | Levels 1–5 cover the whole ZŠ scope, in its order (látky a směsi → částice → prvky a vazby → reakce → oxidy, kyseliny, hydroxidy, soli). |
| **RVP G** (gymnázium), *Chemie*: obecná, anorganická, organická chemie, biochemie | Stavba atomu (orbitaly), periodický zákon, chemická vazba, chemické výpočty, termochemie, kinetika, rovnováha, redox, prvky s- p- d-bloku, uhlovodíky, deriváty, přírodní látky | Levels 2–9. The usual gymnázium sequence is obecná → anorganická → organická → biochemie; we keep it. |
| **Cambridge IGCSE Chemistry (0620)** | States of matter, atoms/elements/compounds, stoichiometry, electrochemistry, energetics, rates, equilibria, acids/bases/salts, Periodic Table, metals, environment, organic chemistry, experimental techniques | Cross-check that nothing a 16-year-old abroad learns is missing; used for the practical/separation topics and the environment lesson. |
| **A-level Chemistry** (AQA/OCR) and **AP Chemistry** (9 units) | Atomic structure, amount of substance, bonding, energetics, kinetics, equilibria and Kc, redox and electrode potentials, periodicity, group 2 and 7, transition metals, organic mechanisms, polymers, amino acids and DNA | Sets the top of the course ("pre-university"). Topics such as Kc, Ka, pH of weak acids, buffers, Hess's law, reaction mechanisms and electrode potentials come from here. |

Principles for the order:
1. **Macro before micro.** Learners first observe substances (level 1), then explain them with particles (level 2).
2. **Atoms before bonds, bonds before names.** Nomenclature (level 3) needs oxidation numbers, which need electronegativity and bonding.
3. **Equations before moles, moles before calculations.** Level 4 builds the whole quantitative toolkit that levels 5–9 use.
4. **Acids and bases twice.** Descriptive (Arrhenius, pH, salts) in level 5, quantitative (Ka, buffers) in level 6, as in the ZŠ → gymnázium spiral.
5. **General chemistry before descriptive chemistry.** Redox, energetics and equilibrium (level 6) come before the chemistry of the elements (level 7), which then uses them to explain *why* elements behave as they do.
6. **Organic before biochemistry.** Biomolecules are explained through their functional groups.

## Detailed syllabus

Each lesson lists its **core content**. Content agents must cover every bullet and must not use concepts from later lessons without saying so.

### Level 1 – Svět látek
*Goal: see the world as made of substances, work safely, handle mixtures and solutions.*

- **l1-1 Co je chemie a jak bezpečně pracovat** – what chemistry studies (substances and their changes); physical vs chemical change (first intuition); chemistry around us; lab equipment (kádinka, zkumavka, baňka, nálevka, kahan, stojan, pipeta, odměrný válec); safety rules; GHS hazard pictograms (diagram `lab-safety`); first aid basics; how to heat a test tube.
- **l1-2 Vlastnosti látek a skupenství** – substance vs body (látka × těleso); properties: colour, smell, density (ρ = m/V, simple calculation), melting and boiling point, solubility, electrical and thermal conductivity; states of matter and changes of state (tání, tuhnutí, vypařování, var, kapalnění, sublimace, desublimace); particle view of states (diagram `states`); diffusion and Brownian motion.
- **l1-3 Směsi a chemicky čisté látky** – pure substance vs mixture; homogeneous vs heterogeneous mixtures; suspension, emulsion, foam, aerosol, smoke, fog; alloys; examples from home; elements vs compounds as pure substances (intuition, no atoms yet).
- **l1-4 Jak oddělit složky směsí** – usazování a dekantace, filtrace, odpařování a krystalizace, destilace, sublimace, chromatografie, magnetic separation, centrifuge; choosing the right method; water treatment as an application (diagram `separation`).
- **l1-5 Roztoky a hmotnostní zlomek** – solution = solute + solvent; saturated, unsaturated, supersaturated; solubility and temperature (solubility curve reading); factors speeding up dissolving; **mass fraction** w = m(látky)/m(roztoku), percentages, mixing and diluting calculations (the "křížové pravidlo" can be mentioned as a tip).
- **l1-6 Voda a vzduch** – types of water (destilovaná, pitná, užitková, odpadní, minerální, tvrdá/měkká); water treatment plant and wastewater treatment plant (úpravna × čistička); composition of air (N₂ 78 %, O₂ 21 %, Ar ~0,9 %, CO₂ ~0,04 %); oxygen and combustion, fire triangle, extinguishing; air pollution (smog, ozone layer vs ground-level ozone, acid rain intro).

### Level 2 – Uvnitř atomu
*Goal: explain matter with particles; read the periodic table like a map.*

- **l2-1 Atomy, molekuly a ionty** – particle model; atom, molecule, ion (cation, anion) as names; chemical element vs compound at particle level; chemical symbols and simple formulas (H₂, O₂, H₂O, CO₂, NaCl); index vs coefficient; brief history (Démokritos → Dalton).
- **l2-2 Stavba atomu: jádro a obal** – nucleus (protons, neutrons) and electron cloud; charges and masses of particles; size of the nucleus vs the atom (stadium analogy); Thomson → Rutherford (gold foil) → Bohr → quantum model (short history); atom is neutral: p⁺ = e⁻.
- **l2-3 Protonové číslo, nukleonové číslo a izotopy** – Z, A, N = A − Z; nuclide notation; isotopes (hydrogen: protium, deuterium, tritium; carbon-12/14); relative atomic mass as a weighted average (simple calculation with chlorine); radioactivity intro (α, β, γ), half-life, uses (radiocarbon dating, medicine); ions: counting electrons in ions.
- **l2-4 Elektronové vrstvy a valenční elektrony** – shells (K, L, M, N) and the 2-8-8 model for Z ≤ 20 (diagram `bohr`); valence electrons; octet/stable noble-gas configuration; why atoms form ions (Na → Na⁺, Cl → Cl⁻); ionisation energy intro; electron-dot (Lewis) symbols.
- **l2-5 Orbitaly a elektronová konfigurace** – orbital as probability region; quantum numbers n, l, m, s (overview); s, p, d, f orbital shapes; Aufbau principle (1s 2s 2p 3s 3p 4s 3d …, the diagonal rule), Pauli principle, Hund's rule; box diagrams (diagram `orbitals`); writing configurations incl. noble-gas shorthand; exceptions Cr, Cu; configurations of ions.
- **l2-6 Periodická soustava prvků** – Mendělejev and the periodic law; groups and periods; s-, p-, d-, f-blocks; group names (alkalické kovy, kovy alkalických zemin, chalkogeny, halogeny, vzácné plyny); metals, nonmetals, metalloids (diagram `periodic-mini`); trends: atomic radius, ionisation energy, electronegativity, metallic character; predicting properties from position.

### Level 3 – Vazby a názvosloví
*Goal: explain why atoms bond and name/write inorganic binary compounds.*

- **l3-1 Proč se atomy spojují: elektronegativita** – energy decreases when a bond forms; bond energy and bond length; electronegativity (Pauling scale), trend in the table; ΔX and bond type (nonpolar < 0,4 ≤ polar < 1,7 ≤ ionic); examples.
- **l3-2 Kovalentní vazba a tvary molekul** – shared electron pair; single, double, triple bond; σ and π bonds; Lewis (valence) structures; lone pairs; coordinate (dative) bond (NH₄⁺, H₃O⁺); VSEPR shapes (lineární, trojúhelníková, tetraedrická, trigonální pyramida, lomená) with angles; polar bonds vs polar molecules (CO₂ vs H₂O).
- **l3-3 Iontová a kovová vazba** – ionic bond as electrostatic attraction; ionic lattice (NaCl); properties of ionic compounds (high melting point, brittle, conductive when molten/dissolved); metallic bond (electron sea) and properties of metals; comparison table of covalent/ionic/metallic substances (molecular vs giant covalent: diamond, graphite, SiO₂).
- **l3-4 Mezimolekulové síly a vlastnosti látek** – van der Waals (London/dispersion, dipole–dipole) forces; hydrogen bonds; boiling points of hydrides (H₂O vs H₂S); why ice floats; solubility "like dissolves like"; surface tension.
- **l3-5 Oxidační číslo a chemické vzorce** – definition of oxidation number; rules (element = 0, H = +I, O = −II, F = −I, sum = 0 / = charge); calculating oxidation numbers in compounds and ions; the Czech endings for oxidation numbers I–VIII (-ný, -natý, -itý, -ičitý, -ičný/-ečný, -ový, -istý, -ičelý); writing a formula from a name via the "cross rule" (křížové pravidlo) and back.
- **l3-6 Názvosloví dvouprvkových sloučenin** – oxidy, peroxidy, halogenidy (fluoridy, chloridy, bromidy, jodidy), sulfidy, hydridy, nitridy, karbidy; formula ↔ name in both directions; important trivial names (voda, amoniak, methan, sirovodík/sulfan); common real-world compounds (CO₂, SiO₂, Fe₂O₃, NaCl, CaO).

### Level 4 – Reakce a výpočty
*Goal: write and balance equations, master the mole and chemical calculations.*

- **l4-1 Chemická reakce a zákon zachování hmotnosti** – reactants and products; signs of a reaction; the law of conservation of mass (Lomonosov, Lavoisier); chemical equation meaning (qualitative and quantitative); states (s), (l), (g), (aq); ionic and short ionic equations (intro).
- **l4-2 Vyčíslování chemických rovnic** – coefficients vs indices; step-by-step method (metals, nonmetals, H, O last); balancing combustion; balancing by oxidation numbers is postponed to l6-1 (mention it).
- **l4-3 Typy chemických reakcí** – syntéza (slučování), rozklad, substituce (nahrazování), podvojná záměna (incl. precipitation, neutralisation); exothermic vs endothermic (intro); fast/slow; reversible reactions (intro, ⇌); redox and acid-base as families (details later).
- **l4-4 Látkové množství a molární hmotnost** – the mole, Avogadro constant N_A = 6,022·10²³ mol⁻¹; n = N/N_A; molar mass M, n = m/M; calculating M from the table; molar volume of an ideal gas V_m = 22,4 dm³/mol (STP) and the ideal gas equation pV = nRT (for gymnázium); mass percentage of an element in a compound; empirical formula from composition.
- **l4-5 Koncentrace roztoků a ředění** – molar concentration c = n/V (mol/dm³); preparing a solution of given concentration; conversion between w and c using density; dilution c₁V₁ = c₂V₂; mixing solutions.
- **l4-6 Výpočty z chemických rovnic** – mole ratio from the equation; mass → moles → moles → mass; gas volumes; limiting reagent; yield (výtěžek); worked multi-step problems.

### Level 5 – Kyseliny, zásady a soli
*Goal: recognise, name and use acids, bases and salts; understand pH.*

- **l5-1 Kyseliny a jejich názvosloví** – Arrhenius acid (H⁺ / H₃O⁺); binary acids (HCl, HF, H₂S) and oxoacids; naming oxoacids from the oxidation number (kyselina sírová, siřičitá, dusičná, dusitá, uhličitá, fosforečná, chloristá…); deriving formulas; important acids and their uses; safety with concentrated acids (nejdřív voda, potom kyselina); acid rain.
- **l5-2 Hydroxidy a zásady** – hydroxides (NaOH, KOH, Ca(OH)₂) and naming; alkalis; ammonia as a base; properties and uses (hydroxid sodný in soap making and drain cleaners, hašené vápno); safety (caustic!).
- **l5-3 pH a indikátory** – autoionisation of water, [H₃O⁺]·[OH⁻] = 10⁻¹⁴; pH = −log[H₃O⁺] (strong acids and bases), pOH, pH + pOH = 14; pH scale with everyday examples (diagram `ph-scale`); indicators (litmus, phenolphthalein, methyl orange, universal indicator, red cabbage home experiment).
- **l5-4 Neutralizace a titrace** – neutralisation acid + base → salt + water; ionic equation H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O; heat of neutralisation; acid–base titration procedure (burette, pipette, indicator, equivalence point); titration calculation; titration curve (diagram `titration-curve`); antacids, soil liming.
- **l5-5 Soli a jejich názvosloví** – ways to prepare salts (acid + base, acid + metal, acid + metal oxide, acid + carbonate, precipitation); naming salts of oxoacids (síran, siřičitan, dusičnan, dusitan, uhličitan, fosforečnan, chlornan…); hydrogen salts (hydrogenuhličitan sodný), hydrates (pentahydrát síranu měďnatého); solubility rules; important salts in everyday life (NaCl, CaCO₃, NaHCO₃, sádra, modrá skalice, fertilisers).
- **l5-6 Teorie kyselin a zásad podle Brønsteda** – proton donor/acceptor; conjugate acid–base pairs; amphoteric substances (water, HCO₃⁻); strong vs weak acids and bases, degree of dissociation; hydrolysis of salts (pH of solutions of Na₂CO₃, NH₄Cl, NaCl) – qualitative; Lewis acids (brief mention).

### Level 6 – Energie, rychlost a rovnováha
*Goal: the physical chemistry toolkit — redox, electrochemistry, energy, rates and equilibria.*

- **l6-1 Redoxní reakce** – oxidation = loss of electrons / increase in oxidation number, reduction = gain / decrease; oxidising and reducing agents; half-equations; balancing redox equations by oxidation numbers (including KMnO₄ examples); redox around us (combustion, respiration, rusting, bleaching).
- **l6-2 Elektrochemie: články a elektrolýza** – Beketov reactivity series (elektrochemická řada napětí kovů); displacement of metals; galvanic cell (Daniell, diagram `galvanic`), anode/cathode, salt bridge, standard electrode potential E° and cell voltage; batteries and accumulators (Li-ion, lead-acid), fuel cell; electrolysis of molten NaCl and of aqueous solutions, electroplating, aluminium production (overview), corrosion and protection.
- **l6-3 Termochemie a Hessův zákon** – system and surroundings; exothermic and endothermic (diagram `energy-profile`); enthalpy change ΔH, thermochemical equations; standard enthalpy of formation and combustion; Hess's law and calculations; bond enthalpies; (optional) entropy and Gibbs energy as an outlook.
- **l6-4 Rychlost reakce a katalýza** – rate definition; collision theory and activation energy; factors: concentration, temperature (van 't Hoff rule of thumb), surface area, catalyst (diagram `rate-curve`, `energy-profile` with catalyst); enzymes and car catalytic converters; rate equation and order (intro for A-level).
- **l6-5 Chemická rovnováha a Le Chatelierův princip** – reversible reactions and dynamic equilibrium; Le Chatelier's principle: effect of concentration, pressure, temperature, catalyst (none); Haber–Bosch ammonia and contact process as case studies; everyday examples (carbonated drinks, haemoglobin).
- **l6-6 Rovnovážná konstanta, slabé kyseliny a pufry** – equilibrium constant Kc (expression, meaning of its size, simple calculations); Ka, pKa; pH of weak acids (approximate formula); buffers: composition, how they work, Henderson–Hasselbalch equation, blood buffer; solubility product Ks (brief).

### Level 7 – Chemie prvků
*Goal: descriptive inorganic chemistry, explained with levels 2–6.*

- **l7-1 Vodík, kyslík a voda** – hydrogen: properties, preparation (Zn + HCl, electrolysis), uses, hydrogen as fuel; oxygen and ozone (allotropes), preparation (H₂O₂ decomposition, KMnO₄), oxides (acidic, basic, amphoteric, neutral); water: structure, anomalies, hardness; hydrogen peroxide.
- **l7-2 Halogeny a vzácné plyny** – group 17 trends (colour, state, reactivity, displacement reactions); chlorine (preparation, water disinfection, bleach), hydrogen halides, halides and test with AgNO₃; fluorine in toothpaste, iodine and iodine tincture; noble gases: inertness, uses (He balloons, Ne signs, Ar in bulbs and welding), xenon compounds as a surprise.
- **l7-3 Síra, dusík a fosfor** – sulfur: allotropes, SO₂, H₂SO₄ (contact process, dehydrating effect), sulfates; nitrogen: N₂ inertness, NH₃ (Haber–Bosch), HNO₃, nitrogen oxides and smog, nitrogen cycle and fertilisers; phosphorus: white/red, phosphoric acid, phosphates, eutrophication.
- **l7-4 Uhlík a křemík** – carbon allotropes (diamond, graphite, graphene, fullerenes, nanotubes); CO (toxic) and CO₂ (greenhouse gas, carbon cycle); carbonates and hydrogen carbonates, limestone cycle (vápenec → pálené vápno → hašené vápno → malta), karst; silicon: semiconductors, SiO₂, silicates, glass, ceramics, cement.
- **l7-5 Alkalické kovy a kovy alkalických zemin** – group 1 and 2 trends; reactions with water and oxygen (and storage under oil); flame tests (Li, Na, K, Ca, Sr, Ba, Cu); important compounds (NaCl, NaOH, Na₂CO₃, NaHCO₃, KNO₃, CaCO₃, CaSO₄, MgSO₄); water hardness and its removal; magnesium in the body and in alloys.
- **l7-6 Hliník, železo, měď a přechodné kovy** – aluminium (passivation, amphoteric behaviour, production by electrolysis, recycling); metallurgy: ores, blast furnace (iron from Fe₂O₃ with CO), steel, rusting; copper (conductivity, patina, alloys brass and bronze); zinc, chromium, silver, gold, platinum; properties of transition metals (variable oxidation numbers, coloured compounds, catalysts); coordination compounds basics (ligand, central atom, [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺).

### Level 8 – Organická chemie
*Goal: read, name and predict the behaviour of organic molecules.*

- **l8-1 Uhlík v organice: vzorce a izomerie** – why carbon is special (tetravalence, chains, rings); types of formulas (molecular, structural, condensed/racionální, skeletal); classification of carbon chains; isomerism: constitutional (chain, position, functional), stereoisomerism (cis/trans, optical/chirality intro).
- **l8-2 Alkany a cykloalkany** – homologous series CₙH₂ₙ₊₂; names methane–dekan; IUPAC naming of branched alkanes (main chain, locants, prefixes); properties and trend of boiling points; combustion (complete/incomplete); radical substitution (halogenation); sources: natural gas, crude oil, refining and fractional distillation, octane number; cycloalkanes and conformations (brief).
- **l8-3 Alkeny, alkyny a aromáty** – double and triple bond, naming (-en, -yn), ethene and ethyne; electrophilic addition (H₂, X₂, HX, H₂O), Markovnikov's rule; bromine water test; polymerisation (polyethylene, PVC intro); benzene: structure, aromaticity, electrophilic substitution (nitration, halogenation), important arenes (toluene, naphthalene), toxicity.
- **l8-4 Halogenderiváty, alkoholy, fenoly a ethery** – functional group concept; halogen derivatives (freons and the ozone layer, PVC, teflon); nucleophilic substitution and elimination (intro); alcohols: naming, primary/secondary/tertiary, hydrogen bonding, methanol (toxicity), ethanol (fermentation, effects), glycerol; oxidation of alcohols; phenol; ethers (diethyl ether).
- **l8-5 Karbonylové sloučeniny, kyseliny a estery** – aldehydes and ketones (formaldehyde, acetone), naming, Tollens and Fehling tests, nucleophilic addition (intro); carboxylic acids (formic, acetic, fatty acids), acidity; esters: esterification (and its equilibrium), naming, smells, hydrolysis and saponification (intro to soaps); acid derivatives overview.
- **l8-6 Dusíkaté deriváty, reakční mechanismy a polymery** – amines, amides, nitro compounds (TNT), basicity of amines; summary of reaction types and mechanisms (radical, electrophilic, nucleophilic; substitution, addition, elimination, rearrangement); reagent types (radical, electrophile, nucleophile); polymers: addition vs condensation polymerisation, polyamides (nylon), polyesters (PET), plastics and recycling codes, microplastics.

### Level 9 – Biochemie a chemie kolem nás
*Goal: molecules of life and chemistry's role in society.*

- **l9-1 Sacharidy** – monosaccharides (glucose, fructose; aldoses/ketoses; open-chain and cyclic forms), disaccharides (sucrose, lactose, maltose), polysaccharides (starch, glycogen, cellulose); photosynthesis equation; glycolysis and cellular respiration overview; iodine test for starch; energy in food.
- **l9-2 Lipidy** – fats and oils as triacylglycerols; saturated vs unsaturated fatty acids, trans fats; hardening of oils; saponification and soaps, detergents and how they wash (micelles); phospholipids and cell membranes; steroids (cholesterol); waxes.
- **l9-3 Aminokyseliny a bílkoviny** – amino acid structure, amphoteric character (zwitterion), essential amino acids; the peptide bond; primary, secondary (α-helix, β-sheet), tertiary and quaternary structure; denaturation (cooking an egg); functions of proteins; biuret test.
- **l9-4 Enzymy, vitaminy a hormony** – enzymes as biocatalysts, active site, lock-and-key / induced fit, factors (temperature, pH), inhibition; coenzymes and vitamins (fat- vs water-soluble, deficiencies); hormones (insulin, adrenaline, steroid hormones) as signalling molecules.
- **l9-5 Nukleové kyseliny** – nucleotide structure; DNA double helix, complementary base pairing (A–T, G–C), hydrogen bonds; RNA types; replication, transcription, translation overview; genetic code; mutations; PCR and DNA testing as applications.
- **l9-6 Chemie v životě: léčiva, paliva a životní prostředí** – drugs (aspirin, paracetamol, antibiotics; dose makes the poison), addictive substances; fuels (fossil vs renewable, biofuels, hydrogen), energy content; greenhouse effect and climate change, ozone, acid rain, water pollution; green chemistry principles; recycling; food chemistry (additives, E-numbers); how to think critically about "chemicals".

## Level tests

Each level ends with **Závěrečná výzva** (10–12 questions). Passing (≥ 70 %) awards the level badge and adds the level's symbol element to the album.
