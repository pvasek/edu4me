# Chemie – ideas to make it interesting

## Hooks that work for teenagers

- **Everyday mysteries**: Why does a can of cola explode in the freezer? Why does an ice cube float but a cube of frozen oil sink? Why does a phone battery swell? Why does bread go stale?
- **Sport and body**: lactic acid and cramps, electrolytes in sports drinks, how breathalysers work (redox with dichromate), why altitude training works (haemoglobin equilibrium).
- **Kitchen chemistry**: Maillard reaction on a steak, caramelisation, why baking soda + vinegar fizzes, red cabbage as a pH indicator, why salt melts ice.
- **Tech**: Li-ion batteries, OLED screens, silicon chips, rare earths in phones, hydrogen cars.
- **Big questions**: climate change and CO₂, microplastics, where the elements come from (stars, supernovae, neutron star mergers for gold).
- **Stories**: Marie Curie (and polonium named after Poland), Mendělejev leaving gaps in his table, Haber (fertiliser and poison gas), Heyrovský (the only Czech Nobel prize in chemistry: polarography), Wichterle (soft contact lenses, invented on a children's construction kit at his kitchen table).

## Czech connections

- Jaroslav Heyrovský, Nobel Prize 1959, polarography.
- Otto Wichterle, soft contact lenses and silon (a Czech nylon).
- Antonín Holý, antiviral drugs used against HIV.
- Uranium from Jáchymov, where Marie Curie got the pitchblende to discover radium and polonium.
- Bohemian glass (silicates, colouring with metal oxides), Pilsner beer (fermentation), Karlovy Vary springs (mineral water chemistry).
- Semtex and Czech chemical industry history (Pardubice, Synthesia).

## Safe home experiments

(Each described in the relevant lesson with a safety note.)
- Red cabbage pH indicator (l5-3).
- Chromatography of felt-tip pens with a coffee filter (l1-4).
- Growing salt or sugar crystals (l1-5).
- Invisible ink with lemon juice (l6-1 redox / l4-3).
- Lemon battery with zinc and copper (l6-2).
- Elephant toothpaste with yeast and hydrogen peroxide 3 % (l6-4 catalysis).
- Starch test with iodine tincture on potato and bread (l9-1).
- Soap from oil (only as a demonstration video; NaOH is dangerous).

## Motivation features (beyond the current version)

- **Laboratorní deník**: learners write one sentence per lesson in their own words; spaced review shows it back to them.
- **"Proč?" button**: every summary item expands into a deeper explanation for curious learners.
- **Challenge mode for exam prep**: timed mixed tests in the maturita style.
- **Element of the day** on the home screen with a fun fact.
- **Collectible "reaction cards"**: famous reactions (thermite, Haber–Bosch, photosynthesis) unlocked by lessons.
- **Share a result** as an image (stars + badge) without accounts.
