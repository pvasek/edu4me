# Biologie – syllabus

From "what is alive?" to the level expected at the Czech maturita and at university entrance (A-level / AP Biology). **12 levels, 80 lessons** (6–8 per level), plus a level test and mini-games in every level. Czech only; levels 1–8 for ZŠ (age 12–15, *přírodopis*), levels 9–12 for gymnázium (age 15–19, *biologie*).

**Mathematics.** Ratios, percentages, reading graphs and simple probability on ZŠ; on gymnázium the Punnett square as probability, Hardy–Weinberg (p² + 2pq + q² = 1), growth curves, rates from graphs, surface area : volume, simple statistics (mean, range, a plain-language idea of significance). No calculus.

### Principles for the order

1. **Observe, then look closer, then explain.** Every topic starts from something the learner can see (a leaf, a pulse, a mouldy bread), then the microscope or a measurement, then the model.
2. **Unity before diversity.** What life is, the cell and how we classify come first (level 1); only then the groups of organisms.
3. **Groups in evolutionary order.** Viruses and bacteria → protists → fungi → plants → invertebrates → vertebrates → human. The order itself tells the story of evolution before evolution is named.
4. **From the whole body to the molecule on ZŠ, back up from the molecule on gymnázium.** ZŠ: organism → organ → tissue → cell. Gymnázium: molecule → cell → physiology → populations and the biosphere.
5. **Spiral: ZŠ describes, gymnázium explains.** Each big area is met twice: cell 1 → 9, plants 3 → 11, human 6 → 11, heredity 7 → 10, evolution 7 → 12, ecology 8 → 12.
6. **Chemistry before molecular biology.** Biomolecules and metabolism are taught structurally in chemistry (level 9); biology recaps them in one lesson (b9-1) and then uses them.
7. **Heredity before evolution, evolution before ecology at the top.** You need genes to explain selection, and selection to explain communities. Ecology closes both stages because it integrates everything.

## Detailed syllabus

Each lesson lists its **core content**. Lessons must cover every bullet and must not use concepts from later lessons without saying so. Experiment ideas (*Vyzkoušej si*) are marked ⚗.

### Level 1 – Život a buňka
*Goal: tell living from non-living, look into a cell, and sort the living world.*

- **b1-1 Co je život a jak ho zkoumáme** – signs of life (metabolism, growth, reproduction, response, movement, cells, heredity); biology and its branches; the scientific method: question, hypothesis, experiment with one variable changed, control group, repeating, reading a simple graph; field notebook.
- **b1-2 Mikroskop a pozorování** – lupa and light microscope: parts, magnification = eyepiece × objective, preparing a wet mount, drawing what you see; scale (mm → µm); Hooke, Leeuwenhoek; ⚗ zoom slider: what becomes visible at 10×, 100×, 400×.
- **b1-3 Buňka: základ života** – cell theory; prokaryotic vs eukaryotic (intuitive); plant vs animal cell: membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplasts, wall, vacuole; what each part does (the "cell as a city" analogy and where it fails).
- **b1-4 Od buňky k organismu** – unicellular, colonial, multicellular; specialised cells; tissue → organ → organ system → organism (plant and animal examples); why cells stay small: surface area : volume (⚗ cube size slider); division of labour.
- **b1-5 Jak se organismy živí a rozmnožují** – autotrophs and heterotrophs; producers, consumers, decomposers (first look); sexual and asexual reproduction; life cycles; why sex mixes genes (intuition).
- **b1-6 Rozmanitost a třídění života** – species; binomial names (Linné); hierarchy (říše/doména, kmen, třída, řád, čeleď, rod, druh); the domains and kingdoms used in Czech schools; dichotomous keys; why classification changes (relatedness).

### Level 2 – Mikroorganismy a houby
*Goal: meet the invisible majority of life and how it helps and harms us.*

- **b2-1 Viry: na hranici života** – structure (nucleic acid + capsid, envelope); why they are not cells; how a virus multiplies (attach, inject, copy, burst); examples (chřipka, COVID-19, klíšťová encefalitida, HPV); antivirals vs antibiotics.
- **b2-2 Bakterie a sinice** – prokaryotic cell (nucleoid, plasmid, wall, flagellum); shapes; division and exponential growth (⚗ doubling every 20 min); sinice and oxygen; useful bacteria (soil, gut, yoghurt) and pathogens (angína, borelióza, salmonella).
- **b2-3 Prvoci a řasy** – protists as a mixed group; měňavka, trepka, krásnoočko, mořští dírkonošci; parasites (zimnička – malaria, trypanozoma); řasy from Chlorella to chaluhy; plankton and half the world's oxygen.
- **b2-4 Houby** – hyphae, mycelium, fruiting body; nutrition (saprotrophs, parasites, mycorrhiza); kvasinky, plísně (Penicillium, Aspergillus), houby s plodnicemi; edible vs deadly (muchomůrka zelená) and safe mushroom picking; houby in food and medicine.
- **b2-5 Lišejníky a soužití organismů** – lichen = fungus + alga/cyanobacterium; symbiosis types: mutualism, commensalism, parasitism; lichens as air-quality indicators; mycorrhiza again; root nodules (first look).
- **b2-6 Mikroby a naše zdraví** – how infections spread; hygiene (hand washing), sterilisation, pasteurisation; immunity basics (barriers, white blood cells, antibodies); vaccination and herd immunity (⚗ % vaccinated slider); antibiotic resistance; the microbiome.

### Level 3 – Rostliny
*Goal: understand how a plant is built, feeds itself and reproduces.*

- **b3-1 Mechy a kapradiny** – the move to land (cuticle, vessels, spores); mechy (gametophyte dominant, rašeliník and bogs); plavuně, přesličky, kapradiny; alternation of generations (simplified); Carboniferous forests and coal.
- **b3-2 Kořen, stonek a list** – organs and tissues of a seed plant; root (anchoring, absorption, root hairs); stem (xylem up, phloem both ways); leaf (structure, stomata); transpiration (⚗ "celer v obarvené vodě" as a picture); modified organs (bulb, tuber, thorns, tendrils).
- **b3-3 Fotosyntéza a dýchání rostlin** – word equation of photosynthesis (light, CO₂, water → sugar, oxygen) and respiration; chlorophyll; limiting factors (⚗ light intensity slider → bubbles of oxygen from vodní mor); plants respire day and night; starch test.
- **b3-4 Květ, opylení a plod** – flower parts; pollination (wind, insects; co-evolution with bees); fertilisation; seed and fruit types; seed dispersal; germination conditions (an experiment with controls).
- **b3-5 Nahosemenné a krytosemenné** – conifers (smrk, borovice, jedle, modřín) vs flowering plants; monocots vs dicots; key Czech families with examples (růžovité, brukvovité, bobovité, hluchavkovité, hvězdnicovité, lipnicovité, liliovité); using a key.
- **b3-6 Rostliny a člověk** – crops and their origin, cereals and domestication; medicinal and poisonous plants; trees in the city; invasive plants (bolševník, křídlatka); protected Czech plants; plant growth responses (light, gravity – first look).

### Level 4 – Bezobratlí živočichové
*Goal: see how animal body plans evolved from simple to complex.*

- **b4-1 Jak je stavěno tělo živočicha** – what makes an animal; symmetry (none, radial, bilateral); body cavity and segmentation (intuitive); how body plans got more complex; sponges (houbovci) as the simplest animals.
- **b4-2 Žahavci, ploštěnci a hlístice** – nezmar and medúzy (žahavé buňky, korálové útesy); ploštěnci (ploštěnka, tasemnice); hlístice (roupi, škrkavka); parasites and their life cycles; hygiene as prevention.
- **b4-3 Měkkýši a kroužkovci** – plži (hlemýžď, slimák), mlži (filter feeding, perly), hlavonožci (inteligence chobotnice); žížala (segments, role in soil, Darwin's worms), pijavka.
- **b4-4 Členovci: korýši a pavoukovci** – exoskeleton, moulting, jointed legs; korýši (rak, stínka, krill); pavoukovci (pavouk, sítě, klíště – borelióza and encefalitida, roztoči); stonožky a mnohonožky.
- **b4-5 Hmyz** – body plan (head, thorax, abdomen, 6 legs, wings); complete vs incomplete metamorphosis; social insects (včela, mravenec); pollinators and their decline; pests and disease vectors (komár).
- **b4-6 Ostnokožci a přehled bezobratlých** – hvězdice, ježovky (relatives of vertebrates!); comparison table of all groups; a dichotomous key to invertebrates; where they live in Czech nature.

### Level 5 – Obratlovci a chování
*Goal: follow vertebrates from water to land and understand animal behaviour.*

- **b5-1 Strunatci a ryby** – the notochord and the vertebrate body plan; bezlebeční (kopinatec); fish: gills, swim bladder, lateral line, fins; bony vs cartilaginous fish; Czech fish and fisheries.
- **b5-2 Obojživelníci** – life between water and land; metamorphosis of the frog; skin breathing; žáby, mloci, čolci; why amphibians are endangered (chytrid fungus, ponds).
- **b5-3 Plazi** – the amniotic egg and dry skin; ještěrky, hadi (zmije – venom and first aid), želvy, krokodýli; ectothermy; dinosaurs as reptiles.
- **b5-4 Ptáci** – feathers, flight adaptations (hollow bones, air sacs, keel); eggs and parental care; migration; bird song; Czech birds and feeders; birds as dinosaurs.
- **b5-5 Savci: stavba a rozmnožování** – hair, milk, endothermy, diaphragm, teeth types (⚗ teeth → diet); placenta vs marsupials vs monotremes; mammal brains.
- **b5-6 Savci: přehled** – main orders with Czech examples (hmyzožravci, letouni, hlodavci, zajícovci, šelmy, sudokopytníci, lichokopytníci, kytovci, primáti); adaptations to habitats; domestication.
- **b5-7 Chování živočichů** – innate vs learned behaviour; imprinting (Lorenz), conditioning (Pavlov), insight; communication; territory, courtship, social behaviour; animal welfare.

### Level 6 – Lidské tělo
*Goal: know how your own body works and how to keep it healthy.*

- **b6-1 Kostra a svaly** – bones (structure, growth, joints), skeleton map; muscles (skeletal, smooth, heart), antagonistic pairs; posture, injuries and first aid for fractures.
- **b6-2 Krev a oběh** – blood components and their jobs; blood groups (ABO, Rh) and transfusion; heart (chambers, valves), double circulation; pulse and blood pressure (⚗ pulse before and after exercise as a graph); heart disease prevention.
- **b6-3 Dýchání** – airways and lungs; alveoli and gas exchange; breathing mechanism (diaphragm); smoking and vaping; resuscitation (KPR) basics.
- **b6-4 Trávení a výživa** – digestive tract and glands; enzymes break food down; nutrients, energy (kJ), vitamins, fibre; balanced diet and food labels; eating disorders (with care).
- **b6-5 Vylučování a kůže** – kidneys (filter blood, urine), water balance; skin (layers, sweat, temperature control as negative feedback – first look), UV protection.
- **b6-6 Nervy a smysly** – neuron, reflex arc, brain and spinal cord; senses: eye (structure; optics is in physics, lesson „Oko a optické přístroje“), ear (structure; sound in physics, „Vlastnosti zvuku a hluk“), taste, smell, touch; protecting senses (noise, screens).
- **b6-7 Hormony, rozmnožování a vývoj** – endocrine glands and key hormones; puberty; reproductive systems; menstrual cycle; fertilisation, pregnancy, birth; contraception and STIs (factual, respectful); stages of life.
- **b6-8 Zdraví, závislosti a první pomoc** – healthy lifestyle (sleep, movement, stress); mental health and where to get help; addictive substances and behaviours (alcohol, nicotine, screens); first aid: calling 155/112, recovery position, bleeding, burns.

### Level 7 – Dědičnost a evoluce
*Goal: understand why children resemble parents and how life changes over time.*

- **b7-1 DNA, geny a chromozomy** – the nucleus holds DNA; gene = instruction for a trait; chromosomes, human karyotype (46), sex chromosomes; DNA extraction from a strawberry (as an illustrated practical).
- **b7-2 Mendel a křížení** – Mendel's peas; dominant and recessive alleles; genotype vs phenotype; homozygote, heterozygote; Punnett square for one trait (⚗ cross builder); ratios 3 : 1 and 1 : 2 : 1 as probabilities.
- **b7-3 Dědičnost u člověka** – ABO blood groups (codominance); sex determination; X-linked traits (colour blindness); genetic diseases (cystic fibrosis, Down syndrome); genetic counselling.
- **b7-4 Proměnlivost a mutace** – variation: genetic vs environmental (identical twins); continuous vs discontinuous variation; mutations and their causes (UV, radiation – see physics „Atomové jádro a radioaktivita“); most mutations neutral, some harmful, a few useful.
- **b7-5 Evoluce a přírodní výběr** – Darwin and Wallace; variation + overproduction + competition + inheritance → natural selection (⚗ peppered moth: background colour vs survival); artificial selection (dog breeds, crops); antibiotic resistance as evolution happening now.
- **b7-6 Vznik života a dějiny Země** – age of the Earth, deep time on a calendar; first cells, oxygen revolution, Cambrian explosion, life on land, mass extinctions, dinosaurs, mammals; fossils and how they form; human origins (overview).

### Level 8 – Země a ekosystémy
*Goal: understand the planet life lives on and how living things depend on each other.*

- **b8-1 Stavba Země a desková tektonika** – layers of the Earth; plates and their boundaries; earthquakes, volcanoes, mountain building; continental drift and its evidence (fossils across oceans).
- **b8-2 Nerosty a horniny** – minerals (crystal, hardness – Mohs scale, streak, Czech examples: křemen, živec, slída, kalcit, granát); rocks: igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic; the rock cycle; Czech geology highlights (Český kras, granite of Šumava).
- **b8-3 Půda a voda** – weathering and erosion; how soil forms, soil layers and organisms; water cycle; groundwater; soil and water protection (drought, erosion of fields).
- **b8-4 Ekosystém a potravní vztahy** – ecosystem = living + non-living; food chains and webs; trophic levels; energy flow and the 10 % rule (⚗ pyramid); decomposers.
- **b8-5 Populace a koloběhy látek** – population, community; what limits population size; predator and prey (first look); carbon and nitrogen cycles (simple); bioaccumulation.
- **b8-6 Ekosystémy Česka** – forest (smrková monokultura vs smíšený les, kůrovec), meadow, pond and river, field, city; typical species and their relations; succession on an abandoned field.
- **b8-7 Ochrana přírody a biodiverzita** – why biodiversity matters; threats (habitat loss, invasive species, pollution, climate change); Czech protected areas (národní parky, CHKO); what one person can do; climate change basics (coordination with chemistry and physics).

### Level 9 – Buňka a energie
*Goal: the cell at the molecular level, how it gets energy and how it divides.*

- **b9-1 Chemie života** – elements of life; water and its properties (polarity, hydrogen bonds, solvent, heat capacity); the four macromolecule groups and their roles (recap: chemistry, level „Biochemie a chemie kolem nás“); monomers and polymers; condensation and hydrolysis.
- **b9-2 Prokaryotní a eukaryotní buňka** – detailed organelles (ER, Golgi, lysosomes, ribosomes, cytoskeleton); endosymbiotic theory (evidence); microscopy: light vs electron, resolution, measuring cells with a scale bar; cell fractionation (idea).
- **b9-3 Membrána a transport** – fluid mosaic model; diffusion, facilitated diffusion, osmosis (water potential, ⚗ cell in salt solutions: turgor, plasmolysis, lysis), active transport, endo/exocytosis; SA : V calculations.
- **b9-4 Enzymy a metabolismus** – active site, induced fit, activation energy; factors: temperature, pH, concentration (⚗ rate vs temperature curve); inhibitors; metabolic pathways and ATP as energy currency.
- **b9-5 Buněčné dýchání** – glycolysis, link reaction, Krebs cycle, oxidative phosphorylation as a story of energy (no full structures); aerobic vs anaerobic (lactate in muscles, ethanol in yeast); respiratory quotient (idea).
- **b9-6 Fotosyntéza** – chloroplast; light-dependent reactions (photolysis, ATP, NADPH) and the Calvin cycle; limiting factors with graphs (⚗ light, CO₂, temperature); C3, C4 and CAM plants.
- **b9-7 Buněčný cyklus a signalizace** – cell cycle phases, mitosis step by step, cytokinesis; meiosis and why it creates variation (crossing-over, independent assortment); cell signalling (receptor → response, idea); checkpoints and cancer as loss of control.

### Level 10 – Molekulární genetika a biotechnologie
*Goal: from DNA to protein to modern gene technology.*

- **b10-1 DNA a její replikace** – structure (nucleotides, base pairing, antiparallel strands), Watson, Crick and Rosalind Franklin; semi-conservative replication (Meselson–Stahl idea); genome size and packing.
- **b10-2 Od genu k bílkovině** – transcription, mRNA processing (introns, exons), the genetic code (codon table), translation at the ribosome (⚗ decoder: DNA → mRNA → amino acids); one gene, many proteins (splicing).
- **b10-3 Regulace genů** – why cells differ with the same DNA; lac operon (idea); transcription factors; epigenetics (methylation, identical twins); stem cells and their use.
- **b10-4 Mutace a nádory** – point mutations (substitution, insertion, deletion, frameshift) and their effects on the protein; chromosomal mutations; mutagens; oncogenes and tumour suppressors; screening and prevention.
- **b10-5 Mendelovská genetika do hloubky** – dihybrid cross (9 : 3 : 3 : 1), linkage and crossing-over, sex linkage, multiple alleles, polygenic traits; pedigree analysis; probability rules; chi-square as "is the difference bigger than chance?".
- **b10-6 Populační genetika** – gene pool, allele frequencies; Hardy–Weinberg principle and its conditions (⚗ p and q sliders); genetic drift, founder effect, gene flow; carriers of recessive diseases in a population.
- **b10-7 Biotechnologie** – restriction enzymes, recombinant DNA, insulin from bacteria; PCR and gel electrophoresis (DNA fingerprinting); sequencing and the human genome; CRISPR-Cas9; GMO crops; bioethics (gene therapy, designer babies, privacy of DNA).

### Level 11 – Fyziologie a homeostáza
*Goal: how organisms keep their inner world stable and respond to the outer one.*

- **b11-1 Homeostáza a zpětná vazba** – internal environment; negative and positive feedback; thermoregulation; blood glucose (insulin, glucagon, diabetes types 1 and 2) (⚗ glucose after a meal with and without insulin).
- **b11-2 Výměna plynů a oběh** – gas exchange in fish, insects, plants and humans compared; heart cycle and its control; blood pressure; haemoglobin and the oxygen dissociation curve (Bohr effect, as a graph).
- **b11-3 Trávení, ledviny a osmoregulace** – digestion and absorption (villi, enzymes along the gut); liver; the nephron (filtration, reabsorption, ADH); water balance; dialysis and transplants.
- **b11-4 Neuron a synapse** – resting and action potential (as a graph), myelin and saltatory conduction, the synapse and neurotransmitters; drugs at synapses; brain areas and plasticity; sleep.
- **b11-5 Smysly, svaly a pohyb** – receptors as transducers; rods and cones, colour vision; sliding filament theory (sarcomere), ATP and Ca²⁺ in contraction; fast and slow fibres; training.
- **b11-6 Imunita** – innate vs adaptive immunity; phagocytes, B and T lymphocytes, antibodies, memory cells; primary vs secondary response (graph); vaccines (including mRNA); allergies, autoimmunity, HIV; monoclonal antibodies (tests, therapy).
- **b11-7 Fyziologie rostlin** – water transport (transpiration stream, cohesion–tension), translocation in phloem (source → sink); plant hormones (auxin, gibberellins, ethylene, ABA); tropisms and photoperiodism; plant defences.

### Level 12 – Evoluce a ekologie
*Goal: the big picture: how species arise and how the biosphere works.*

- **b12-1 Důkazy evoluce a fylogeneze** – fossils, comparative anatomy (homologous vs analogous), embryology, molecular evidence (DNA, proteins); reading and building cladograms; the three domains.
- **b12-2 Vznik druhů** – species concepts; allopatric and sympatric speciation; isolation mechanisms; adaptive radiation (Darwin's finches, cichlids); gradualism vs punctuated equilibrium; coevolution.
- **b12-3 Evoluce člověka** – primate evolution; hominins (Australopithecus, Homo erectus, Neanderthals, Homo sapiens); out of Africa; evidence from DNA (Neanderthal genes in us); cultural evolution.
- **b12-4 Populační ekologie** – population growth: exponential and logistic, carrying capacity (⚗ growth model); r- and K-strategies; predator–prey cycles (lynx and hare); estimating population size (capture–recapture).
- **b12-5 Společenstva a sukcese** – niche, competition (competitive exclusion), symbiosis revisited; keystone species; primary and secondary succession; biodiversity indices (Simpson, idea).
- **b12-6 Ekosystémy a biomy** – energy flow quantitatively (productivity, efficiency); carbon, nitrogen and phosphorus cycles in detail; biomes of the world; Czech ecosystems in the European context.
- **b12-7 Člověk a biosféra** – the sixth mass extinction; climate change and its biological effects; conservation biology (protected areas, rewilding, gene banks); sustainability and ecosystem services; what science says and what remains debated.
